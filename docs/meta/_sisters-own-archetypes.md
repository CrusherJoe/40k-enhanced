# Adepta Sororitas — OUR list archetypes & the pick (11E, 2026-07-26)

> **NOT an opponent doc.** This records **our own** Sisters list-building conclusion:
> the 5 detachment archetypes we evaluated for the October event, the recommended
> Hallowed Martyrs list, the rev-7 corrections that apply to every build, and the
> one gap none of them close. Leading `_` sorts it to the top of `docs/meta/`.
> Strategic thesis (unchanged): **Sisters win the MISSION, not the slugfest.**

**Deep-sim head-to-head (3 finalists vs the 8-archetype current meta):**
- **Hallowed Martyrs:** Fav vs T'au/Custodes/Orks; even/grind vs Necrons/Nids/AdMech;
  **UNFAV vs Emperor's Children + DA-Ravenwing** (the two losses). **The only FORGIVING
  one** (the Miracle-Dice engine is a variance crutch) and it handles the elite-melee
  gap *least-badly structurally*: W1 bodies WASTE Fulgrim/Norn multi-damage (S14 D6+1
  kills only ~4), casualties FEED the dice + Blood-of-Martyrs engines, Sacresants +
  2× Arco tarpits waste enemy activations → out-TEMPO the melee (can't win it).
- **Army of Faith:** BEST at out-HOLDING the un-killable; reliability guarantees the
  plan; but HIGH piloting and the double-spend economy runs dry vs Fulgrim. Higher
  ceiling if you master dice-budgeting.
- **Champions of Faith:** no-invuln-armour EXECUTIONER (~24-33 dmg one-shots
  Hammerhead/Monolith/Kataphron/Ravager); FOLDS vs invuln/−1Dmg (Void Dragon, Fulgrim);
  body-light, no martyrdom scaling. Take only if the field is armour-heavy.

**Penitent Host reconsideration:** the 5-0-0 netlist is really a **Sacred-Champions
Sacresant-brick** (4++ / Hospitaller FNP5+ / −1-wound / Miracle-saves), NOT a
vow-powered alpha (vows only buff PENITENT models). It's a **durable sidegrade, not
an upgrade** — the 4++ floor neutralizes the AP-3/elite meta, but it's WORSE vs the
two things that actually beat Sisters (focused AP-2 VOLUME still clears ~a brick/turn
with no metabolize; M6 foot can't pin mobile/fall-back shooters). **Keep Hallowed
Martyrs;** log the brick as a lower-variance ALT for a grind-and-hold player expecting
an AP-3/elite field.

## Recommendation — Hallowed Martyrs (Priority Assets, ~1975/2000)
The only forgiving engine; wins the board on Priority Assets (out-OC: 2× BSS OC40 +
Triumph + Insidiants + sticky Simulacrum objectives); melta one-rounds killable
anchors via Castigator +2 AP / cover-strip / Vahl re-roll.

- **Vahl + 3 Paragon Warsuits** (multi-meltas) — the one hard punch + the elite-melee anchor
- **Triumph of St Katherine** — the modular buff-loom (2 auras/round + a guaranteed-6 die)
- **2× Castigator** (BATTLE CANNON — Blast + Ignores Cover beats the cover most targets sit in; take in pairs for softening redundancy)
- **Celestian Insidiants (10)** + Palatine [Through Suffering, Strength] + Rhino — serial quarry-assassins
- **Sanctifiers (10, meltagun)** + Ministorum Priest — self-sustain + Simulacrum dice
- **Battle Sisters (10, Simulacrum)** + Canoness [Chaplet of Sacrifice]
- **Battle Sisters (10, Simulacrum)** + Imagifier [Saintly Example] + Immolator
- **Celestian Sacresants (5)** + **2× Arco-Flagellants (5)** — disposable tarpits (highest-EV point vs the melee-heavy top meta)

**Practice priority before the Oct GT: Emperor's Children + DA-Ravenwing** (the two
losses) — play both as "tarpit + out-score," never as fights.

## rev-7 corrections (apply to EVERY Sisters build)
- **ALL core bodies are W1** — including every Sacresant/Sacresant variant. The 4++
  "anchor" now dies one-for-one past the invuln (no multi-wound cushion).
- **"Shield of Faith" is NOT a named rule** — the 6++/5++/4++ is baked into each
  datasheet's Invuln save; don't cite a blanket army rule.
- **Durability (corrected):** core body = T3 W1 **Sv3+ + 6++**. Save vs AP: AP-1→4+,
  AP-2→5+, AP-3→6+, AP-4+→6++ floor. **NEVER saveless**; Miracle Dice can buy a clutch
  save. ⇒ **high-AP is WASTED on Sister chaff** (6++ floor makes AP-3=AP-4; D6 melta
  into W1 is triple-overkill) — what kills Sisters is **AP-1/-2 VOLUME + W1** (one dead
  body per unsaved wound). Sisters BLEED but don't evaporate. (Same logic as the Knights
  5++ correction, applied to the 6++.)
- **Old Champions + Sacred Champions combo is LIKELY ILLEGAL** (both REVEREND —
  verified on Sacred Champions). Re-check any two-detachment pairing for keyword clashes.
- **Bringers of Flame = 2DP** (MFM has per-faction DP costs).
- **BSData carries NO stratagem text** — strat details are faction-pack/codex-sourced.
- **Multi-detachment enhancement cap = 3** (+ a possible Orators off-cap trick to 4 —
  confirm with the TO). **Transport cap = 6** (a 10-Dominion can't be delivered; use
  5-melta Rhino bombs). Wargear taxes STILL exist (Paragon MM +10, Retributor MM +5,
  Dominion meltagun +5; flamer free).
- **Sisters melta is INEFFICIENT into multi-wound elite INFANTRY** (no spillover: 6
  melta ≈ 2.8 Custodian / 3.8 Black Knight / 4.7 Kataphron) — save melta for W10+
  single-model bricks; clear elites with volume/Blast.

## The universal gap — elite Fights-First melee
**None of the 5 archetypes is favoured** into elite Fights-First MELEE (Emperor's
Children/Fulgrim, Custodes, Tyranid Norns, Terminator hammer bricks). This is the
structural Sisters weakness — glass infantry die before swinging. The fresh build's
deliberate answer is **not to win the fight but to survive it**: **W1 wastes their
multi-damage, Paragons + a durable anchor absorb, Sacresant/Arco tarpits eat
activations, and casualties feed the martyrdom/dice engine** → out-TEMPO and
out-SCORE the melee army on the mission. Corollary from the Knight work: Sisters
answer melee-ALPHA (deep-strike/jump) armies BETTER than Knights (massed AP-1/2 +
Miracle Dice shoots the drop down as it lands), but stay hard into durable-out-OC
(Space Wolves, C'tan) and focused AP-2 volume (Necron 54-gauss).
