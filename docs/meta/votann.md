# Leagues of Votann — Dêlve Assault Shift + Hearthguard Covenant (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

> **Judgement Tokens DO NOT EXIST in 11E.** The Votann army rule is now **Prioritised
> Efficiency** — do not model the old 10E escalating Judgement-token damage bonus.

## The list & tapestry
A durable, mid-range army whose real teeth are a **deep-striking melee maul-swarm**. Run **Dêlve
Assault Shift** with a **Hearthguard Covenant** angle (note: **Hearthband** is a newer detachment
in the feed — grade separately when a list posts).
- **Cthonian Beserks** — the danger unit: **[ANTI-VEHICLE 3+] MELEE**. They wound my Knights on
  **3s regardless of Strength**, and they arrive via **deep strike + 9" charge** into the
  **no-melee-invuln Questoris (3+ save only)**. This is invuln-negation by keyword — exactly what
  kills Knights.
- **Land Fortresses** — heavy vehicle platforms; the fire-support anchors (kill 2 to gut the list).
- **W1 chaff / Hearthkyn bodies** — screening and objective-holding volume.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Cthonian Beserk maul-swarm** — [ANTI-VEHICLE 3+] MELEE into a Questoris with **no melee
  invuln**. Rotate (shooting-only) can't stop it; only the **Cerastus Lancer's 4++ full invuln**
  resists. The deep-strike + 9" charge is the alpha to deny.
- **Land Fortresses** — anti-tank fire platforms; killable (no invuln → my Volcano/thermal delete
  them).
- Note: no Judgement-token damage ramp in 11E, so the Beserks are the whole anti-Knight story.

## Anti-Sister threats
- **Beserk melee** butchers glass T3 W1 Sv3+/6++ — Anti-Vehicle doesn't matter vs infantry, but
  the raw melee volume/AP does; the deep-strike charge lands before Sisters can kite.
- **Land Fortress + Hearthkyn volume** shreds W1 bodies at range.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: COIN-FLIP / winnable.** The anti-Knight is a single deep-striking melee threat plus
two killable gun platforms — deny the one and delete the others.

- **Deciding factor:** whether I deny the **9" Beserk charge**. If they get the maul-swarm into a
  Knight with no counter, [Anti-Vehicle 3+] cracks it; if I screen the drop and force a failed/
  blocked charge, the list has little else that hurts me.
- **HEIST:** **screen + terrain + reserves to deny the 9" charge** (never expose a lone Knight to
  the drop); **Lancer (4++) counter-charges** the Beserks (the one piece that tanks their melee);
  **kill the 2 Land Fortresses early** with Volcano/thermal (no invuln); and **shred the W1
  bodies** with volume guns while **out-OC**-ing the board.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
**Verdict: hard.** Melee butchers glass, and Votann out-bodies on the objective.
- **Plan:** treat the Beserk drop as a shooting problem — massed AP-1/2 bolter+melta VOLUME +
  Miracle Dice to gun the maul-swarm down as it lands (W1 bodies waste their multi-damage; melta
  the Land Fortresses). Tarpit/screen the deep strike, don't fight the Beserks, and win on tempo/
  OC where possible. This is a lean-loss to be respectable in, not to solve.

## Data caveats
- Judgement Tokens are gone in 11E (army rule = Prioritised Efficiency) — the single most
  important correction; any older Votann note assuming the token ramp is stale.
- **Hearthband** is a new detachment name in the feed and is un-analysed; the verdict above is for
  the Dêlve/Hearthguard maul-swarm build. Grade Hearthband when a list is available.
- Beserk profile (Anti-Vehicle 3+ melee, deep strike) from votann_fp.txt + the meta bank;
  detachment stratagem text is FP-sourced.
- Verdicts assume a Valourstrike Knight list (Rotate = one Knight 4++/phase, shooting only —
  useless vs the Beserk charge) + ~1 Cerastus Lancer.
