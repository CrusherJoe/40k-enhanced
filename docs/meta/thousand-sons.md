# Thousand Sons — Grand Coven (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

The reputation is "psychic mortal-wound spam"; the 11E reality is a THROTTLED psychic army whose
only real Knight-killer is Magnus. Stats live-verified vs BSData/wh40k-11e (`tsons11.json` +
`tsons_fp.txt`). This is one of the **FAVOURABLE** Knight matchups — the rare list Knights should
expect to beat.

## The list & tapestry
- **Grand Coven** psychic engine, but **1 Ritual per turn army-wide** in 11E → **~5 mortal wounds
  per turn via Doombolt**, NOT the 10E MW-spam. The psychic MW is capped, not a firehose.
- **Rubric Marines** = **warpflamer** anti-infantry (AP0/-1 torrent) — deletes bodies, bounces off
  T11-12/Sv3+ vehicles.
- **Scarab Occult** AP-2 volume, but only **S4** — wounds my T11-12 on 5s/6s.
- **Magnus the Red** (**T11 W16 2+ / 4++**, melee **S16 AP-3 [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]**) — the ONLY
  model in the list that can kill a Knight.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Magnus, and essentially only Magnus:** S16 AP-3 Dev melee into the no-melee-invuln fight phase
  is the one thing that threatens a Questoris. Everything else is throttled MW (~5/turn Doombolt),
  warpflamers (bounce off armour/hull), and S4 volume (wounds T11-12 on 5+).

## Anti-Sister threats
- **Warpflamers + Scarab AP-2 volume** delete T3 W1 bodies (S4 wounds T3 on 2s, AP-2 strips the
  3+); ~5 MW/turn chips characters. This is a real body-clearing list against glass infantry —
  harder for Sisters than for Knights.

## How Imperial Knights fare
- **FAVOURED ~55-60/40.** Deciding factor: **the only Knight-killer is Magnus** — refuse or kill
  him and nothing else cracks the Ion Shield + T11-12. The psychic MW is throttled (~5/turn),
  Rubric warpflamers and S4 Scarab volume can't hurt vehicles. HEIST: **refuse/kill Magnus** (keep
  a Knight out of his charge, or Lancer-assassinate T11 W16 4++ — target-rich for the shock lance:
  S20 AP-3 D8 [LANCE] + Crushing Impact MW that bypasses the 4++), **keep Ion Shields up** vs the
  chip, and **out-OC on Priority Assets** — a mid-model psychic list can't out-body OC10 Knights.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
- **~EVEN.** Deciding factor: durable core answers the throttled MW, but warpflamer + AP-2 volume
  clears W1 bodies faster than Sisters would like. **Plan:** Hallowed Martyrs — **melta the Scarab
  Occult / Rubrics** (kill the AP-2 and warpflamer sources), **refuse Magnus** (tarpit or out-range
  him; W1 bodies waste his S16 D-melee multi-damage anyway), feed the dice engine, and out-score on
  bodies. Coin-flip that tilts on discipline vs the flamer volume.

## Data caveats
- **Grand Coven psychic Ritual throttle (1/turn) and stratagem text are FP/codex-sourced** —
  BSData carries no stratagem text; unit stats (incl. Magnus T11 W16 2+/4++, S16 AP-3 Dev)
  verified vs `tsons11.json`.
- The "~5 MW/turn" Doombolt figure is the throttled-engine estimate, not a per-source cap; confirm
  the exact Ritual/psychic wording against the current FP before a hard game.
- Verdicts robust (verified rules); prevalence from a small survivorship-biased top-table sample,
  provisional.
