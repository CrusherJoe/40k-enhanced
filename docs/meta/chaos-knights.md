## The list & tapestry
A **mixed big-gun + fast-hound** build stacking two detachments (3 DP):

- **Bastions of Tyranny** — a **Knight Tyrant** (big Chaos Knight) shooting
  detachment. Rule *Annihilate the Unworthy*: Knight Tyrant attacks vs a
  **battle-shocked** unit get **+1 to hit**. Stratagems: *Rune-Cursed Stronghold*
  (FNP 5+ vs mortals), *Pitiless Focus* (**fall-back and still shoot**), *Intimidating
  Reminder*, *Hate-Filled Dominion* (re-roll a weapon's **Attacks**). Enhancement
  *Pterrorshade Rookery* = +6" detection range on a target (**strips my Hidden/Gone-to-Ground**
  screens — but Knights are VEHICLE and can't be Hidden anyway, so this bites Sisters/allies).
- **Houndpack Lance** — a **War Dog** (Armiger-tier) fast melee-pack detachment.
  *Imperious Advance* lets War Dogs **move through models and terrain** (Super-Heavy
  Walker doesn't apply — but War Dogs are plain WALKER); *Animalistic Rage* =
  fights-on-death when a War Dog is destroyed; *Harrying Hounds* (8" version).

**Chaos-specific threads (vs a loyalist Knight mirror):**
- **Dreadblades** — mercenary Chaos Knight characters bolted on for melee/utility.
- **Dark rites / warp** — e.g. Knight Abominant *Warp Storms* (D3 MW to each enemy
  unit within 9" end of Movement) + Volkite Combustor (30" A12 S12 AP0 D3, Ignores
  Cover, **Dev Wounds**); assorted FNP/invuln dark-rite buffs the loyalist mirror lacks.
- Same **Cerastus/Questoris chassis** both ways: Chaos Cerastus shock lance = A5 WS2+
  **S20 AP-3 D8 [LANCE]** + Shock Charge/Tank Shock (identical to my Lancer).

## Anti-Knight threats
It's a near-**mirror**: the same guns and the same **S20 AP-3 D8 melee lances** that
crack my no-melee-invuln Questoris — pointed at me. Warp Storms + Volkite Dev-Wounds
add invuln-bypassing chip my loyalist mirror doesn't have. War Dogs move through
terrain/models to force melee; the Knight Tyrant out-ranges/out-shoots if I let it
sit. The real leverage is **battle-shock synergy** (Annihilate the Unworthy +1 hit
vs shocked) — but shocking a durable Knight is hard.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict (preliminary): favourable / user-confident.** It's the **same chassis I
know cold** — I bring the same Volcano/thermal guns, the same Cerastus Lancer 4++
assassin, and I match their durability. Deciding factor is the usual Knight-mirror
lever: **whoever assassinates the other's linchpins first + wins the Ion-Shield /
Rotate exchange**. My Lancer (4++ full invuln, S20 D8 LANCE + Crushing Impact ~3-4
invuln-ignoring MW/charge) one-rounds their Knight Tyrant / Abominant / Dreadblade
characters just as it does the Lion. Chaos Knights are reportedly **struggling in
the current meta** (thin bodies, no horde/OC engine — the same low-model trap I have,
without a clear edge). **HEIST (sketch):** duel their big Knight with my Lancer +
Volcano focus, screen the War Dog pack with Armigers/kiting (plain WALKER = no
fall-back-and-shoot for the hounds), Rotate the focused Knight, and out-score on
Purge. **Not deep-verified — confirm before relying on it.**
