# Space Wolves — Librarius Conclave Terminator/Wulfen deathstar (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

SW's competitive shell is NOT Thunderwolf cavalry — it's a **deep-striking Terminator/Wulfen
DEATHSTAR** run out of the Librarius Conclave (pick-a-Discipline psychic support). Stats
live-verified vs BSData/wh40k-11e (`sw11.json` + `sw_fp.txt`; thunder hammer confirmed **Melee A4
WS3+ S8 AP-2 D2 [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]**). It out-bodies and out-OCs while cracking Knights in melee.

## The list & tapestry
- **5× 10-model 2+/4++ Terminator/Wulfen bricks** that Deep Strike — a wall of bodies with an
  invuln, led by Logan / Ragnar / Librarians.
- **8 Assault Terminators** with **thunder hammers: S8 AP-2 D2 [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]** → **~12-14
  into a Questoris per charge**, and the Dev crit-wounds skip the invuln.
- **Sternguard "Fusillade"**: bolt volume with **[ANTI-VEHICLE 5+]** + Dev Wounds that punch the
  Ion Shield.
- Librarius Conclave = one psychic Discipline per round (buffs/re-rolls on the bricks).
- **Half the melee is duds** — Wulfen S5, Blood Claws S4 bounce off T12; the hammers are the threat.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Thunder hammers into the no-melee-invuln fight phase:** S8 AP-2 D2 + Devastating Wounds crack
  a Questoris (only a 3+ save in melee) ~12-14/charge, mortals skipping the 4++/5++.
- **Sternguard Fusillade** [ANTI-VEHICLE 5+] + Dev punches the Ion Shield at range.
- **Out-bodies / out-OCs:** 50 Terminator/Wulfen bodies with 4++ vs my ~6-9 models — I lose the
  board even in games I win the kill-race.

## Anti-Sister threats
- The same hammers + 4++ bricks **butcher glass Sisters** (T3 W1) on the drop; 50 bodies with an
  invuln out-body and out-OC the mission, and it's durable enough that AP-2 volume clears it only
  slowly — the two things Sisters are worst into (durable + out-OC).

## How Imperial Knights fare
- **UNFAVOURABLE.** Deciding factor: **melee-into-fight-phase Dev hammers + out-bodied/out-OC'd** —
  Rotate can't stop the hammers and I can't out-body 5 bricks. HEIST: **Lancer (4++ melee)
  counter-charge** to tank a brick, **assassinate the load-bearing characters** (Logan / Ragnar /
  Librarians → collapse the psychic buffs and leadership), **screen the deep strike** (deny the 9"
  charge), and out-mission on Take-and-Hold where possible. Accept underdog status.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
- **UNFAVOURABLE.** Deciding factor: durable 4++ bricks that also out-OC — this hits both Sisters
  weaknesses (can't out-slug the invuln wall, can't out-body it) and the hammers vaporize glass
  infantry. **Plan:** Hallowed Martyrs — tarpit the drop (Arco/Sacresants waste hammer attacks on
  W1 bodies), feed the dice/martyrdom engines, contest with jump speed, and grind AP-1/2 volume
  into the 4++ (68% of 4++ fails) to shave a brick a turn while out-scoring on tempo. A hard grind,
  not a clean win.

## Data caveats
- **"Beastslayer" is a labeling artifact** — the live competitive detachment is **Librarius
  Conclave**; ignore any "Beastslayer" tag on the source list.
- **Wulfen encoded at S5** (BSData) — the Wulfen/Blood Claw melee is genuinely low-Strength and
  bounces off T12; the threat is concentrated in the thunder hammers, not the body count's damage.
- Librarius Conclave psychic + SW stratagem text is FP/codex-sourced; unit stats verified.
- Verdicts robust (verified rules); prevalence from a small survivorship-biased sample, provisional.
