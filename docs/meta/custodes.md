# Adeptus Custodes — Lions of the Emperor + Might of the Moritoi (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

> The **"so few models"** elite — the INVERSE of the horde. Tanky per-model but low-wound and
> low-count (~40 models total). Quality-per-model does NOT beat a durable, high-OC, high-firepower
> Knight army. (HeeYaw Game 5: user won 66-58 in 4 turns, a tabling-in-progress.)

## The list & tapestry
- **Lions of the Emperor** detachment — Martial Mastery (crit on 5+) + Martial Ka'tah stances;
  a melee-quality buff engine on a small elite core.
- Bodies are **T6-7, 2+/4++** but only **W2-3 (W3-4 top end)**, and the whole army is **~40
  models**. Tanky-per-model, but not many models and not many wounds each.
- Anti-Knight is **THIN on both axes**: shooting ≈ **one Caladius** (twin arachnus 48" A4 S12
  AP-3 D6+2 TL, ~6/turn into a Rotated Knight); the rest is **S7 guardian spears** (wound my
  T11-12 on **5+** — negligible).
- **Might of the Moritoi** (2nd detachment) is **WALKER / Dreadnought-ONLY** — if the list has no
  Dreadnoughts, it does **nothing** (a for-fun build; it was dead weight in the HeeYaw game).

## Anti-Knight threats
- **One Caladius** (S12 AP-3 D6+2 TL) — the only meaningful gun; ~6/turn into a Rotated Knight.
  Volcano/thermal deletes it.
- **Melee is high-quality but low-S / low-count** — S5-7, few models. Even with Lions crit-5+ and
  Ka'tah stances they **cannot gang-kill a W26-28 Knight** in the fight phase.
- **No invuln-negation, no volume, no horde-OC** — the three things that actually kill Knights are
  all absent. This is why it's the favourable inverse of the horde.

## Anti-Sister threats
- **2+/4++ melee elites** (guardian spears / misericordia, Lions crit-buffed) out-fight glass
  Sisters model-for-model.
- Low model count, though, means Sisters can **out-body** them — the invuln is the real barrier.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: FAVOURABLE.** My Knights barely take damage; I grind their few low-W bodies off and
out-hold the board.

- **Deciding factor:** volume of anti-infantry fire vs their tiny model count + OC. My guns chew
  W2-3 bodies faster than they can threaten a W26-28 hull, and my **OC10 out-holds their OC2×few**
  on Purge/Disruption.
- **HEIST / gun discipline:** do **NOT Volcano a W3 Custodian** (overkill waste) — pour **volume
  guns** (Avenger A18 / RFBC / plasma) into bodies, and save **Volcano/thermal for the Caladius +
  characters**. **Lancer Sweep** (A10 S10 AP-2 D3, wounds T6 on **2+**) chews Custodian squads and
  the list is **target-rich for Lancer decapitation** (Blade Champion, 2 Shield-Captains).

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
**Verdict: favourable.** Sisters out-body the low model count and their **softened melta cracks
the 4++**.
- **Plan:** out-body on objectives (cheap OC, jump speed, sticky tags), and use **softened melta**
  (Castigator +2AP / cover-strip / Vahl re-roll) to punch through the **4++** on the anchors —
  note the softening only helps vs the invuln, and multi-wound melta into W2-3 elites lacks
  spillover, so clear the rank-and-file with **volume/blast** and reserve melta for the Caladius /
  characters. Win the MISSION on OC.

## Data caveats
- Custodes W-values: bodies W2-3 (up to W3-4 on heavier profiles); the "so few models" read holds
  regardless. Stats from custodes_fp.txt + the HeeYaw-game bank.
- The favourable verdict assumes NO Dreadnoughts (Might of the Moritoi inert). A Moritoi list built
  *around* walkers (Dreadnought anti-Knight melee) would need re-grading — flag if one posts.
- Verdicts assume a Valourstrike Knight list (Rotate = one Knight 4++/phase) + ~1 Cerastus Lancer.
