# Orks — Kult of Speed / More Dakka & Green Tide (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

Two poles that bracket the faction. A take-all-comers Knight (or Sisters) list
must straddle the **melta↔flamer / speed↔body** axis they define. Stats verified
vs BSData/wh40k-11e (`Orks.json`); detachment rules + stratagems from the Orks
faction pack v1.1.

---

## Pole 1 — Kult of Speed / More Dakka (Speedwaaagh!): fast dakka

### The list & tapestry
Mobile gunline on wheels; **out-mobiles + chips a Knight down** rather than
tabling.

| Unit | Stats | Threat weapon |
|---|---|---|
| Deffkoptas ×~18 (6/unit) | T6 W4 4+ **M12"** | Kopta rokkits S9 **AP-2** D3 Blast, Twin-linked — anti-elite/tank volume |
| Flash Gitz ×2 (10 ea) | T5 W2 4+ | Snazzgun S6 **AP-1** D2 A3 Heavy, Sustained 1 |
| Tankbustas | T5 W2 4+ | Rokkit S9 **AP-2** D3 Blast — **pops transports** |
| Kill Rig ×2 | **T10 W16 3+** | Wurrtower S12 AP-3 D6 Torrent; Stikka kannon S12 AP-2 **Anti-Vehicle 2+** |
| Wazdakka | T8 W10 3+ **M14"** | Psyko-gatler S6 AP-2 D2 **A12** |
| Big Mek Shokk Gun | T5 W5 4+ | Shokk attack gun S9 **AP-4** D6 Blast |
| 3× Trukk, 3× Gretchin | T8 W10 4+ / chaff | mobility + action/OC bodies |

**Tapestry threads (the multipliers — cut these):**
- **Speshul Ammo** (1CP): a shooting unit's ranged weapons gain **[ANTI-MONSTER 4+] + [ANTI-VEHICLE 4+]** — wounds my T11-12 on **4s regardless of Strength**. This is the invuln-adjacent Knight-killer: reliable wounds that only my 5++/4++ Rotate stands behind.
- **Mobile Dakkastorm** (1CP): after a Speed Freeks/Trukk unit shoots, **+2 S** to all attacks into a hit unit for the phase — stacks the whole mob's fire onto one Knight.
- **Adrenaline Junkies / turbo**: Speed Freeks **advance-and-shoot + advance-and-charge**; nothing pins them.
- **Dust Trails / Evasive Manoova**: on-demand Benefit of Cover + bail-to-reserves = hard to focus down.

### Anti-Knight threats
Massed **AP-2 mid-S dakka** (18 koptas + gitz + tankbustas) delivered on M12-14
chassis, sharpened by Speshul Ammo (Anti-Veh 4+) and Mobile Dakkastorm (+2 S).
The rokkits **pop transports** — Immolators are not safe ablative walls. A focused
turn ≈ **one Knight chunked/killed per turn**. Objective game = **Disruption**:
koptas + trukks + grots out-manoeuvre and do actions.

### How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: Knights UNDERDOG.** Deciding factor: they **out-body and out-mobile me
on Disruption** (M12-14 everywhere, grots/trukks for actions) while Speshul Ammo +
volume grinds ~1 Knight/turn — I can't out-score a faster army that also wounds
through Toughness. **HEIST:** kill the force-multiplier hulls early (2 Kill Rigs,
Wazdakka, Big Mek) with Volcano/thermal; Rotate the focused Knight; refuse the
action/mobility race I can't win and win the **kill-race on tempo** — delete the
soft W2-W4 shooty mobs (each kill = OC + dakka removed) faster than they chip me.
Lock **Purge the Foe** (I can't play their action game). Respectable, not favoured.

### How Adepta Sororitas fare
Bleed-but-survive. AP-1/-2 volume kills W1 girls, but the 6++ floor + Miracle Dice
+ transports hold; my melta is over-invested here (T6/W2-4 bodies). Answer their
speed with my own fast scorers + shoot the koptas/gitz down; win the mission, not
the chase. Harder than Green Tide is easy — this is the winnable Ork pole for Sisters.

### Data caveats
Kult of Speed stratagems (Speshul Ammo / Mobile Dakkastorm / Dust Trails) are
faction-pack-sourced (not in BSData). Deffkopta count/loadout is list-dependent
(~18 is the dakka-max build). **Freebooter Krew** (a piratical Ork detachment —
loot-objective + Kaptin buffs + Boardin' Rush board-control) also appeared in the
feed; not analysed in depth (it's a board-control/loot variant, not a distinct
anti-Knight threat profile). Rate the pole, not the exact roster.

## Pole 2 — Green Tide (Taktikal Brigade): the body mountain

### The list & tapestry
**~100-165 bodies.** The known Knight **AUTO-LOSS**, and a top all-comers list
(grinds down nearly everyone).

| Unit | Stats | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **5-6× Boyz (20 each)** | **T5 W1 Sv5+ OC2** | Choppa S4 AP-1 A3; Nob power klaw S9 AP-2 D2; Painboy → **FNP** |
| Ghazghkull + Makari | **T6 W10 2+** | Gork's Klaw S14 AP-3 D4 |
| Warbosses / Bigbosses | T5 W6 4+ | Power klaw S10 AP-2 D2; buff auras |
| Painboy ×3 | T5 W3 5+ | grants **FNP** to the mob |
| Kommandos, Stormboyz, Gretchin | chaff/jump | infiltrate + screens + OC |

**Key durability thread: Mob Mentality** = Boyz get a **5++ while the unit is 10+
models** (6++ otherwise) — this caps my AP-1/-2 anti-horde math (~20-33% fewer kills
than plain Sv5+, so the matchup is WORSE than raw stats suggest). **There is NO
Boyz-return mechanic** (the "come back" line belongs to Speedwaaagh! / Evasive
Manoova, not the Tide).

> **"The Greenback" (~165 bodies):** 6× Boyz(20)=120 + 22 Zodgrod-Gretchin (Scouts 9" + +1 Hit) + Stormboyz + Kommandos (Snikrot backfield-raid) + 3 Painboys (FNP on 3 mobs). The ceiling: out-OCs any army 240+, holds through T3-4.

### Anti-Knight threats
A **melee tsunami** — 100+ choppas + power klaws + Warbosses + Ghaz — into the
no-melee-invuln Questoris fight phase (my Ion Shield is ranged-only; bare 3+ in
melee, only the Lancer's 4++ holds). But the real loss condition is **OC, not
damage**: ~200-330 Objective Control vs my ~65. Take and Hold.

### How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: known AUTO-LOSS — ACCEPT it.** Deciding factor: **8-9 Knights physically
cannot clear + out-body + out-OC 100-165 disposable bodies.** The horde-hardened
list C (Valiant + Warden + Crusader + 3 Warglaives) clears only ~135/157 chaff over
5 turns and the OC race is unwinnable → ~coin-flip vs the 100-body build, slight-Ork
favour vs the Greenback. **No list tweak solves this** (I over-engineered anti-horde
tech chasing it). **This is a FEATURE of 40k, not a list flaw** (every army has a
hard counter) — quantify it, respect it, hope to dodge it, don't warp the list to
"fix" the unsolvable. **HEIST (marginal):** thin the tide at range with anti-horde
volume (Avenger A18, Valiant Conflagration, Warden Heavy Flamer — auto-hit/blast beats
the 5++ better than AP), Super-Heavy Walker to fall-back-and-still-shoot (the horde
can't tarpit big Knights), kill Ghaz with the S24 harpoon R2, and steal secondaries.
Note **TITANIC units cannot Fire Overwatch (15.08)** — no auto-hit flamer on the charge.

### How Adepta Sororitas fare
The Green Tide is every low-model army's worst matchup, but Sisters clear bodies
better than Knights: heavy bolters (AP-1 D2 murders W1 Boyz), Castigator battle cannon
(Blast, Ignores Cover — beats the cover the tide sits in), flamers/Torrent, and Blast
volume. Still can't out-body — thin at range, screen, never get tied up in melee, win
on secondaries + positioning. Disposition = Disruption/Death Trap (kill blobs in
terrain, dodge the OC race), not out-hold.

### Data caveats
Mob Mentality (5++ at 10+ models) verified vs the faction pack — earlier plain-Sv5+
math was ~20-33% too generous. Boyz points/unit shift with MFM updates (feed shows
current pricing); rate the pole. FNP source is the Painboy (list-dependent count).
