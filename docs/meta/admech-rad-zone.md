## The list & tapestry
- **Detachments:** **Lords of the Forge** (+) **Rad-Zone Corps**. **Disposition:** typically Take and Hold / Priority Assets. Record: top-table 5-0/4-1 finisher.
- **Army rule — Doctrina Imperatives:** selectable Command-phase buffs (+1 to Hit or +1 to Wound / ballistic-or-weapon-skill) — the multiplier that turns Kataphron volume into anti-Knight lethality.
- **Rad-Zone Corps rule:** **chip mortal wounds + battle-shock pressure** (auras/stratagems that inflict MW and force battle-shock on nearby enemies). **⚠ It does NOT reduce Toughness** — the 10E-style −1T "rad" effect is GONE in 11E. Its job is incremental MW + shutting off my abilities via battle-shock, not making me easier to wound.
- **The anti-Knight bricks (verified BSData):**
  - **Kataphron Breachers** — **Heavy arc rifle** 30" A2 S8 AP-2 **D3 [Anti-Vehicle 4+, Rapid Fire 2]** → wounds ANY Knight on **4s regardless of Strength** (invuln-negation by keyword); at half-range (RF2) that's A4 each.
  - **Kataphron Destroyers** — **Heavy grav-cannon** 30" A4 S6 AP-1 **D2 [Anti-Vehicle 2+]** (wounds a Knight on 2s!) or plasma culverin (supercharge 36" A4 S8 AP-3 D2 Hazardous).
  - Bricks are 3-6 Kataphrons (T6 W4 3+ or T5 W3), **buffed with Lethal Hits** (doctrina / stratagem) → auto-wound on crit-hits stacks with the Anti-Vehicle wound floor. Net ≈ **~1 dead Knight per turn** of concentrated Kataphron fire.
- **The body game:** a wide, cheap chassis (Skitarii, Kataphrons, Serberys, Dunecrawlers) that **out-bodies and out-OCs** my 6-9 models on Take and Hold, plus sticky-objective play.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Kataphron Anti-Vehicle X+ guns** are the killer — heavy arc rifle **Anti-Vehicle 4+**, heavy grav-cannon **Anti-Vehicle 2+** wound my T11-12 regardless of Strength, stripping the "high Toughness" defence; AP-1/-2 forces the 68%-failing 5++ and Lethal Hits punches through on crits.
- **Volume + Lethal** across the bricks ≈ 1 Knight/turn; Rotate saves only one, and there are enough shots to threaten more.
- **Out-OC / out-body:** the real loss condition — AdMech wins the board with cheap sticky OC while I'm removing bricks.
- **Rad-Zone chip MW + battle-shock:** minor damage, but battle-shock can switch off my abilities (and could deny a Rotate turn) — annoyance, not a wound-multiplier.

## Anti-Sister threats
- Kataphron AP-1/-2 volume (D2/D3) chews T3 W1 Sisters — bodies die one-for-one; the arc rifle's D3 and grav's D2 are efficient into W1.
- High-value melta/plasma is **wasted overkill on W1 chaff** (6++ floor) — Sisters bleed to volume, not to any single big gun.

## How Imperial Knights fare
- **UNFAVOURABLE.** Deciding factor: the Kataphron **Anti-Vehicle X+** guns negate my invuln by keyword (~1 dead Knight/turn) while the cheap wide chassis **out-bodies and out-OCs** me on the objective game — I lose the board even in games I win the kill-race. THE HEIST: **focus-delete the Kataphron bricks EARLY** — they have low Toughness/Wounds per model (T5-6 W3-4, **no invuln**), so Volcano/thermal/Avenger volume removes a brick fast; kill the brick before it kills a Knight, Rotate the focused Knight, screen to deny the RF2 half-range step (keep them shooting at A2 not A4), and bank mobility secondaries. Accept the OC loss and play for a respectable score — this is a loss-leaning matchup.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
- **EVEN (winnable).** Deciding factor: the Kataphron bricks have **NO invuln**, so Sisters **melta cracks them** (the softened-melta chain: strip cover via Immolator Purge & Cleanse, +2 AP via Castigator, Vahl re-roll → the Vahl+Paragon hammer one-rounds a brick; math ≈ Kataphron ~4.7 damage per 6 melta shots per model, so target whole bricks with the concentrated stack). And the Rad-Zone chip/battle-shock has no Toughness-reduction teeth. THE PLAN: win the MISSION — melta the no-invuln Kataphrons + Dunecrawlers, clear Skitarii chaff with volume/blast, out-body isn't available so **out-TEMPO** on objectives and secondaries, metabolize the AP volume (Hallowed Martyrs martyrdom), and hold with sticky/Simulacrum bodies. It's a grind she can win on points.
