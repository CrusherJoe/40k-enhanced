# Dark Angels — Ravenwing swarm / Deathwing brick / Lion speeders (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

DA are the three-headed matchup: a fast **Ravenwing/Darkflight** speeder-bike swarm, the
classic **Deathwing** invuln-brick, and a **Lion + Speeder** hunter build. Stats live-verified
vs BSData/wh40k-11e (`da11.json`) + the separate DA faction pack (`da_fp.pdf` — Unforgiven /
Deathwing / Ravenwing). All three share the DA problem for the underdog: they either out-OC
you, out-mobility you, or hunt your linchpin.

## The list & tapestry
**Build A — Ravenwing / Darkflight Pursuit (Company of Hunters + Dark Age Arsenal).** A
speeder-bike SWARM: **~90 M12" bikes** + Black Knights + speeders. **27 Catechism plasma
shots** with **Devastating Wounds**; Black Knight melee is **[ANTI-VEHICLE 4+]**. The whole army
**Falls Back and still shoots** (I can't pin it), and it **out-OCs on Disruption** with 90 fast
bodies. This is the hard pole.

**Build B — Deathwing brick (Unforgiven / Librarius Conclave).** 3× **Deathwing Knight** bricks:
**T5 W4 2+ / 4++, −1 Damage, OC1**, Mace of Absolution S6 AP-2 D2 A4 [ANTI-VEHICLE/MONSTER 4+] +
mortal output, each Deep-Striking with a Librarian + Ancient (2+/4++). Un-tableable but **thin
board control** (a 5-brick is only OC5, ×3). See also `dark-angels-deathwing-brick.md`.

**Build C — Lion + Speeders.** **Lion El'Jonson** (T9 W10 4++) as a melee decapitator (Fealty
one-rounds a no-melee-invuln Questoris in the fight phase) behind a **Storm Speeder
Thunderstrike + Land Speeder Vengeance** gun package. Both speeders M14" T8/T9 W10/11 **3+/6++
OC3**, Deep Strike; Nightforged Battery enh maximizes safe supercharge.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Build A:** 27 Dev-Wound plasma → crit-wounds become **mortals that skip the 5++/4++**;
  Black Knight [ANTI-VEHICLE 4+] wounds my T11-12 in melee regardless of Strength; 90 bikes
  out-OC; fall-back-and-shoot denies my pinning. **Force-MULTIPLIER = Storm Speeder
  Thunderstrike:** after it shoots it MARKS a Monster/Vehicle → every friendly ranged attack
  gets **+1 to WOUND** vs it (turns 10 Hellblasters + 3 plasma speeders from wounding a Knight
  on 4+ to 3+). Land Speeder Vengeance "Storm of Vengeance" = reactive out-of-sequence plasma.
- **Build B:** the maces' [ANTI-MONSTER/VEHICLE 4+] + mortal output crack a Questoris in the
  no-melee-invuln fight phase; but there is **no decapitation target** and OC is thin.
- **Build C:** the **Lion** himself — Fealty melee one-rounds a Questoris (3+ save only in melee).

## Anti-Sister threats
- **Build A:** 27 Dev plasma + torrent/bolter volume deletes T3 W1 bodies; **M12" fall-back-and-
  shoot means M6" foot Sisters can never pin or trade** — mobility loss is the real killer.
- **Build B:** Deep-Striking 2+/4++ melee bricks grind glass infantry to paste — the archetype
  that **98-31'd** the user's Sisters at HeeYaw (3 Deathwing bricks).
- **Build C:** speeder plasma + Lion melee overkill; the marks make each shot land harder.

## How Imperial Knights fare
- **Build A — UNFAVOURABLE / hard-loss (~20-40%).** Deciding factor: **Dev-Wound plasma + [ANTI-
  VEHICLE 4+] melee bypass both Ion Shield AND the 4++**, and 90 fall-back-shooting bikes
  out-OC/out-mobility me on Disruption. HEIST: kill the **Storm Speeder Thunderstrike FIRST**
  (a Volcano deletes T9 W11) to collapse the +1-to-wound mark; screen the drop; Rotate the
  focused Knight; accept the OC/board loss and win on kills + secondaries where possible.
- **Build B — FAVOURABLE on the mission.** Deciding factor: **Deathwing are OC1** — durable ≠
  scoring. HEIST: **out-OC** with OC10 Knights (no decapitation target exists — the Lancer only
  chips a W4 brick, per the 31-98 corollary), refuse the melee, kill the killable (Sternguard,
  Repulsor, characters), win Take-and-Hold on bodies.
- **Build C — FAVOURABLE (user is 8-0 vs Lion).** Deciding factor: **the Cerastus Lancer HUNTS
  the Lion** — S20 AP-3 D8 [LANCE] + Crushing Impact ≈ 17 unsaved into T9 W10 4++ → **one-rounds
  him even under FNP** (M14 + Valourstrike advance makes the T2 catch). HEIST: Lancer-assassinate
  the Lion on sight; Volcano the Storm Speeder to blank the mark engine; out-OC the rest.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
- **Build A — UNFAVOURABLE.** One of the two Sisters losses (with EC). No fix by list-tuning:
  M6" foot can't catch a fall-back-shooting M12" swarm, and Dev plasma clears bodies. **Plan:**
  Hallowed Martyrs — tarpit + out-SCORE, never fight; feed casualties to the dice/martyrdom
  engines; contest with jump units (Seraphim/Zephyrim); melta the speeders. Practice this as a
  named loss to be respectable in, not a game to win.
- **Build B — HARD-LOSS lesson (the 98-31).** Can't out-slug 2+/4++ Deep-Strike bricks. **Plan:**
  **out-OC** (cheap OC2 bodies flood what OC1 bricks can't reach), screen the Deep Strike, treat
  transports as expendable delivery, melta still works on W4 (multi-damage removes a model per
  failed 4++ — judge melta by target WOUNDS/model), win the mission on points.
- **Build C — even/grind.** Shoot the speeders down with AP-1/2 volume + Miracle Dice; screen the
  Lion (no Lancer to hunt him) and out-position on the objective game.
