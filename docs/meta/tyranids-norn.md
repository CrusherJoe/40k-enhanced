> The **5-Norn monster-mash**: five Norn Emissaries/Assimilators anchored by OC15
> objective-holders, self-healing, psychic-MW support. Faced live at HeeYaw Game 4
> (user WON 81-75) — a genuine coin-flip for Knights, a grind for Sisters. It is a
> **removal race, not a C'tan-style un-killable wall**: only the 3 Assimilators

## The list & tapestry
- **Detachments:** **Talons of the Norn Queen** (+) **Assimilation Swarm**. **Disposition:** **Take and Hold.** Record: user beat it 81-75.
- **Army rule:** Synapse + **Shadow in the Warp** (once/game force ALL my units to battle-shock test — can deny my Rotate on a key turn).
- **Assimilation Swarm rule — Feed the Swarm:** Harvester units **Regenerate** a friendly Tyranids unit each Command phase (heal wounds OR return a destroyed model) + Norn Unstoppable Monstrosity D3/turn.
- **Talons rule — Higher Imperatives → Protean Purpose:** each Norn re-picks its **Singular Purpose** ONCE per battle (turn-1 pick, swap once).
- **Key units (buffed ceiling):** 5 Norns (M10 T11 W16 Sv2+ OC5):
  - **3× Assimilator** — **4++** via Synaptoprescience; Toxinjecter Harpoon (12" shoot to MARK a Knight → **+2 to charge it**), then talons A6 S9 AP-2 D3 + harpoon melee A4 S12 AP-3 D6+1.
  - **2× Emissary** — **NO invuln** (base 2+ save); psychic tendril 18" S12 AP-3 D6 [Melta 2] + [Anti-Character 2+].
  - **Neurotyrant** (heal-aura / synapse / warlord) + **6 Zoanthropes** (Warp Blast S12 AP-3 D6+1 Lethal Hits psychic + MW) + chaff.
- **Singular Purpose menu:** **HUNTER** = re-roll Hit AND Wound vs one named unit all game (~doubles a Norn's damage into a chosen Knight); **ANCHOR** = while on an objective, **FNP 5+ AND Objective Control 15**.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Norn harpoon melee (Assimilators):** the Toxinjecter Harpoon marks a Knight (**+2 to the charge**), then A4 S12 AP-3 D6+1 harpoon + A6 S9 AP-2 D3 talons in melee — into the no-melee-invuln Questoris (bare 3+; Ion Shield is ranged-only). Two Norns on one Knight = dead Knight.
- **HUNTER re-roll Hit+Wound** roughly doubles a Norn's output into whichever Knight it names.
- **Psychic:** Zoanthrope Warp Blast (S12 AP-3 D6+1 Lethal Hits + MW) and Emissary tendrils bypass/pressure the shooting-only invuln.
- **OC15 anchor:** my Knight is OC10 — **I CANNOT out-OC an OC15 anchor**; on Take and Hold the objective becomes a KILL-RACE (physically remove the Norn to take it).

## Anti-Sister threats
- Norn melee (S12 AP-3 D6+1) and psychic (S12 AP-3) are high-AP/high-Damage — **wasted overkill on T3 W1** Sisters (6++ floor makes AP-3 = AP-4; D6 into W1 is triple-overkill). Sisters BLEED to volume, not to Norns.
- The real Sister problem is **tempo + the OC15/FNP anchors + regen** — the swarm heals back what she chips, and OC15 out-holds her bodies.

## How Imperial Knights fare
- **COIN-FLIP → hard (genuine, verified — user won 81-75).** Deciding factor: their shooting is only moderate (~13 S12 AP-3 shots), and **durability is SPLIT** — only the 3 Assimilators have 4++; the 2 Emissaries + Neurotyrant + Zoanthropes have **NO invuln**, so my Volcano (S18 AP-5) ~**one-shots an Emissary**. THE HEIST (Monolith-style removal race, NOT a C'tan wall): focus-kill the no-invuln bodies at range first (Emissaries → Zoanthropes → Neurotyrant), grind the 4++/FNP/heal Assimilator anchors, **Lancer-assassinate** (target-rich; a Lancer charge ≈ 21 dmg kills a Norn) — and REFUSE the melee gang: kite M10-14, never let 2+ Norns pile one Knight. Win on kills + secondaries + removing enough anchors — you win by SUBTRACTION, not by out-bodying OC15×several.
- **Positioning:** hold the SAFE objectives from a base-touch ~3" out (170×105mm oval); decline the one small footprint an OC15 anchor squats. Keep gun-Knights out of harpoon-charge range; feed the Warglaive/Lancer forward.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
- **GRIND (playable, not favoured).** Deciding factor: high-AP Norn attacks are wasted on W1 so Sisters don't evaporate, but the **regen + OC15 anchors** mean she can't out-hold and can't out-kill the heal-loop. THE PLAN: win the MISSION — melta the **no-invuln Emissaries** (the one meltable target on the board; Hammerhead-class math) and shred Zoanthropes/chaff with volume+blast, out-tempo on secondaries, use Miracle Dice for clutch saves, and DON'T contest an OC15 footprint (it's a kill-race she can't win) — take the objectives the anchors AREN'T on. Bank the board she can hold, concede the ones she can't.
