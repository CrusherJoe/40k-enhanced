# Drukhari — Skysplinter Assault + Exhibition of Slaughter (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

> Army rule **Power From Pain** in 11E is a **pain-TOKEN economy** — units bank/spend Pain tokens
> to Empower (verified: dark-lance/prow profiles reference "spend 1 Pain token to Empower") —
> **NOT** an escalating turn-by-turn buff table. Don't model it as the old 10E ladder.

## The list & tapestry
A **glass-cannon** raider army: fast, fragile, hits hard from range and in melee but folds to
return fire. Run **Skysplinter Assault** with an **Exhibition of Slaughter** angle.
- **13× dark lances** — **36" S12 AP-3 D6+2** (verified drukhari_fp): the anti-tank backbone.
  S12 wounds my T11-12 well and AP-3 forces the Ion Shield, but it's *ordinary* wounding — **no
  haywire, no Anti-Vehicle X+, no Devastating Wounds** in the lance package.
- **Incubi + Exhibition of Slaughter** melee with **[LETHAL HITS]** — but the Lethal/decapitating
  keywords are **NON-VEHICLE** (verified in the meta bank): they **do not trigger on my Knights**
  (Vehicles) at all.
- Fast skimmers (Raiders/Venoms/Ravagers), splinter anti-infantry, deep/flank threats.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **13 dark lances (S12 AP-3)** — the only real Knight-killer, and it's **thin invuln-bypass**:
  no haywire and no Dev Wounds means my **5++ Ion Shield (Rotate → 4++ on one)** stays fully
  relevant. It's volume-AP, not invuln-negation.
- **That's essentially it.** The melee (Incubi/Exhibition Lethal Hits) is **non-Vehicle → does
  NOT touch Knights**. This is the rare list where the scary keyword sails right past me.

## Anti-Sister threats
- **Splinter + lance volume** and **Incubi/Exhibition Lethal-Hits melee** (which DOES apply to
  Sisters — they're not Vehicles) shreds T3 W1 Sv3+/6++ bodies.
- Speed + deep strike lets them pick fights and score before Sisters reposition.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: EVEN / slightly my way.** The durability mismatch runs in my favour: Drukhari are a
glass army with thin anti-Knight tools, and their signature melee keyword can't even hurt me.

- **Deciding factor:** attrition. I out-durable them massively — their bodies **DIE to my guns**
  with no invuln to save them — while their only anti-Knight (13 lances, ordinary S12 AP-3) is
  slowed by my Ion Shields. The real risk is **losing Reconnaissance tempo**: they're faster and
  will out-manoeuvre for board/secondary VP even as I win the kill-race.
- **HEIST:** grind their skimmers/lances off with volume + Volcano/thermal, Rotate the focused
  Knight, and **don't cede the recon game** — screen my movement, contest their fast objective
  grabs, and out-OC where I can. No character-assassination is required (no load-bearing
  multiplier); just out-trade and hold tempo.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
**Verdict: coin-flip.** Glass-on-glass — whoever gets the alpha and controls tempo wins.
- **Plan:** massed bolter+melta VOLUME + Miracle Dice to shoot their fragile skimmers/Incubi
  down as they commit (their bodies die as easily as mine). Screen the deep strike, tarpit the
  Incubi charge, and win the MISSION on OC/tempo — don't get out-manoeuvred on secondaries.

## Data caveats
- The Drukhari BSData 11E lib pull was thin (`drukhari11_lib.json` is a stub); stats above lean
  on `drukhari_fp.txt` (dark lance profile verified there) + the meta bank. Exhibition-of-Slaughter
  stratagem/keyword text is FP-sourced.
- "Lethal Hits are non-Vehicle" is the load-bearing fact for the EVEN verdict — reconfirm against
  the current EC... (Exhibition) datasheet if a new list posts.
- Verdicts assume a Valourstrike Knight list (Rotate = one Knight 4++/phase) + ~1 Cerastus Lancer.
