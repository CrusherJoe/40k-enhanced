# Necrons — Cursed Legion & Awakened Dynasty (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

> Necrons ≈ **~20% of the current top-table meta** — the single most-represented
> faction. Two live archetypes, both UNFAVOURABLE→hard-loss for Knights and hard
> for Sisters, plus an older Monolith build that has faded. All three share the
> Necron third axis: **Reanimation Protocols** (each unit heals wounds every
> Command phase → chip is refunded, you must kill OUTRIGHT) layered on invuln /
> −1 Damage monster resilience that partly negates "AP is king."

---

## The list & tapestry

### Archetype A — Cursed Legion (18-Lokhust gauss spam) — UNFAVOURABLE / hard-loss
- **Detachment:** Cursed Legion. **Disposition:** typically Purge / Recon. Record: top-table 5-0/4-1 finisher.
- **Army rule:** Reanimation Protocols (D3 W/unit/Command phase). **Detachment rule — Cold Fervour:** buffs the gauss line **+2 Strength** (base gauss cannon S5 → **S7**) plus Lethal/re-roll enablers.
- **The engine:** **18 Lokhust Destroyers** (T6 W3 3+) = **54 gauss-cannon shots at 24" A3 S7 AP-2 D2 [Lethal Hits]** (base 24" A3 S5 AP-2 D2 Lethal, buffed to S7). That is ~22 dead Sister-bodies/turn and a genuine Knight-chewer: AP-2 forces the 68%-failing 5++, Lethal Hits auto-wound on crit-hits, and D2 removes W1 chaff one-for-one.
- **Force-multiplier at ceiling:** a **C'tan Void Dragon** anchors it (T11 W16 3+, spear 12" S8 AP-3 D6+2 **[Anti-Vehicle 2+]** — wounds ANY Knight on 2s, heals off my hulls) + reanimation buff-characters (Nekrosor / Plasmancer / Technomancer).

### Archetype B — Awakened Dynasty (= triple-C'tan spam) — COIN-FLIP Knights / HARD-LOSS Sisters
- **Detachment:** Awakened Dynasty. **Disposition:** **Take and Hold.** Record: 5-0-0 top-table.
- **Detachment rule — Command Protocols:** +1 to Hit for led units — **minor**; the list is carried by the models, not the rule.
- **The three C'tan (all un-killable — 4++ and/or −1 Damage and Reanimation):**
  - **Nightbringer** — T11 W16, Gaze of Death 18" A(D3) S12 AP-3 D6+3; scythe melee S14 AP-4 D6+2 A6 Dev Wounds.
  - **Void Dragon** — T11 W16, spear **[Anti-Vehicle 2+]** (12" S8 AP-3 D6+2; strike Melee S12 AP-4 D6+2), heals off vehicles.
  - **Transcendent C'tan** — T11 W16 **4++**, Seismic Assault / crackling tendrils.
- **Support:** Szeras, Nekrosor (warlord), 2× Skorpekh Destroyers, Imotekh. All C'tan are **OC4 and mobile (M ~10", FLY)**.

### Archetype C — Obeisance / Monolith spam (older, faded) — brief note
- 3× **Monolith** (T13 W22 Sv2+ OC8, death ray S12 AP-4 D6+1) + Silent King. Slow (M8"). Effectively un-killable for Sisters; **Knights CAN table a Monolith** (S18 Volcano / S24 harpoon; Monolith IS **Titanic** → shieldbreakers' [Anti-Titanic 4+] apply). Superseded by the two faster, invuln-heavier builds above.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Cursed Legion gauss wall:** 54× S7 AP-2 D2 Lethal Hits. VOLUME through the 5++ + Lethal crit-wounds = the killer; enough shots to threaten TWO Knights in a phase (Rotate only saves one).
- **Void Dragon spear [Anti-Vehicle 2+]:** wounds any Knight on 2s regardless of Strength — invuln-negation by keyword; and it heals off the Knight it damages.
- **C'tan melee (Awakened Dynasty):** Nightbringer scythe S14 AP-4 D6+2 A6 Dev Wounds one-rounds into the no-melee-invuln Questoris (3+ only in the Fight phase; Ion Shield is ranged-only).
- **Out-OC:** Reanimation + army-wide OC + sticky play means I lose the board even in games I win the kill-race.

## Anti-Sister threats
- **The 54-gauss firehose is the exact profile that kills Sisters:** AP-2 VOLUME + D2 into T3 W1 Sv3+/6++ → ~22 dead bodies/turn (AP-2 → 5+ save, fails 67%; each unsaved wound removes a body). High-AP/high-D C'tan attacks are WASTED overkill on W1, but the gauss line is perfectly shaped.
- **Distortion Fields / +1 AP auras** shred W1 chaff further; **C'tan D6-damage guns** are inefficient into Sisters (save melta-return by not clustering).

## How Imperial Knights fare
- **Cursed Legion — UNFAVOURABLE / hard-loss (~20-30% game).** Deciding factor: 54 AP-2 Lethal shots out-damage my Rotate (saves ONE Knight) while Reanimation refunds my chip and the Void Dragon out-durables + out-OCs. THE HEIST: this is a **removal race, not a wall** — focus-delete the killable enablers with Volcano/thermal (Void Dragon is only 3+ base until Transcendent; kill the reanimation characters — Nekrosor/Plasmancer/Technomancer — to stop the heal-loop), Rotate the focused Knight, screen the Void Dragon off my hulls, and accept the OC loss while banking mobility secondaries. Realistic result is a loss; play for a respectable score.
- **Awakened Dynasty — COIN-FLIP (the MORE winnable Necron build).** Deciding factor: it's **Take and Hold**, and my **OC10 Knights out-HOLD the C'tan's OC4** — I win on POINTS, not tabling (you cannot table 3× W16 4++/−1Dmg Monsters). THE HEIST: out-OC the objectives, use the **Cerastus Lancer's 4++ full invuln to DUEL one C'tan** (D8 [LANCE] + Crushing Impact ~3-4 MW bypass the invuln), screen the Void Dragon (Anti-Veh 2+ + hull-heal), never feed the melee gang, and score the board while they can't remove enough of my bodies.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
- **Both builds — HARD.** Cursed Legion is the harder: the 54-gauss volume clears ~a brick/turn and Sisters have no Toughness/save answer to it — martyrdom (Hallowed Martyrs) metabolizes some of it but it still grinds. Awakened Dynasty is a **HARD-LOSS**: there is **no melta target** (C'tan un-killable, D6-melta wasted on… nothing meltable), and Sisters **can't out-hold** — the C'tan are OC4 AND mobile (M10 FLY), so they contest my objectives while Distortion Fields +1 AP shreds my W1 bodies. THE PLAN vs both: win the MISSION not the slugfest — spend melta only on the killable Lokhusts/support/reanimators, out-position the slow-to-arrive kill, bank Miracle Dice on clutch saves, and out-tempo on secondaries. Expect to be behind.
