# Space Marines (Salamanders) — Forgefather's Seekers flamer-brick (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

Torrent/flamer aggression on durable-but-invulnless hulls. **Corrects the old
10E-sourced doc** (`space-marines-salamanders-flamer-brick.md`): flamestorm cannon
is **S6 AP-2 D2** (was mis-stated S6 AP-1 D1), the detachment is **Forgefather's
Seekers** (NOT Librarius Conclave), and Land Raiders have **no invuln**. Stats from
BSData/wh40k-11e (`sm11.json`) + SM faction pack v1.1.

## The list & tapestry
Vulkan-led all-flamer brick that advances into 12" and torches everything, riding
2× Land Raider Redeemer for delivery + ablative wounds.

**Tapestry threads (the multipliers):**
- **Vulkan's Quest** (detachment rule): every Adeptus Astartes **ranged weapon gains [ASSAULT]** (advance + still fire the flamers) and **+1 Strength within 12"** → flamestorm cannon becomes S7, pyreblaster S6.
- **Immolation Protocols** (2CP): a unit's **Torrent weapons gain [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]** for the phase — auto-hitting flamers convert crit-wounds to mortals that **skip my Ion Shield**. The one turn the flamer wall bypasses my invuln.
- **Vulkan Forgefather**: mark an enemy unit → friendly **Torrent/Melta re-roll Wounds** into it (stacks with Oath of Moment: re-roll Hits + +1 Wound army-wide vs one unit — mono-Salamanders gets the +1).
- **Armour of Contempt** (1CP): −1 AP defensively on a targeted unit.

## Anti-Knight threats
Two real ones. **(1) The multi-meltas** (2× Land Raider + any Outriders): S9 AP-4 D6
Melta 2 = my only serious anti-Knight gun, but only ~4 shots and needs <9" for the
Melta bonus. **(2) Immolation-Protocols flamers**: normally AP-1/-2 Torrent bounces
off my hull, but the Dev-Wounds turn chips mortals through the Ion Shield. The
melee (Vulkan's Spear Dev Wounds, fists) threatens the no-invuln Questoris fight
phase if I let the brick reach me. **Respect the 12" bubble** — inside it, +1 S
flamers + gauntlets pour auto-hitting Dev-capable fire.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: FAVOURABLE.** Deciding factor: **the Land Raiders are a paper tiger —
T12 W16 2+ but NO invulnerable save.** My Volcano Lance (S18 AP-5) wounds T12 on
2s and the 2+ armour does nothing against AP-5 → **each Land Raider one-/two-shots
off the board**, and with them die the delivery + half the flamer output. The rest
is a fragile T4 W2 infantry brick that my anti-infantry volume (Avenger A18, RFBC,
plasma) shreds, and it has almost no way to crack a Rotated Knight at range
(flamers are AP-1/-2 into my 3+/5++; only the Dev-Wounds turn or the multi-meltas
bite). **HEIST:** **kill the 2 Land Raiders first** (Volcano/thermal/harpoon),
then **respect the 12" flamer bubble** — kite M5-M12 infantry with my range
(56-80"), never park a Knight inside 12", and bait Immolation Protocols onto a
Rotated/cheap target. A mirror flamer brawl is theirs; a ranged standoff is mine.
My Towering Knights still get Benefit of Cover off ruin walls, so their flamers eat
−1 to hit anyway. Lock **Purge the Foe** (out-position + score kills — I delete
their durable-looking-but-invulnless list faster than they can close).

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
This is the "AP is KING" archetype for Sisters and the case **against** an
aggressive flamer build (Bringers of Flame / Purge): a mirror flamer brawl LOSES —
they out-flamer me and I'm the fragile T3 W1 side, auto-dying inside 12" with no
cover save vs Torrent/Ignores Cover. Answer = **melta the Land Raiders** (multi-melta
Retributors, meltagun Dominion halves, Paragon multi-meltas — AP-4 cracks the 2+;
no invuln to fear), fight from transports (ablative), never park bodies inside 12",
and contest their Recon scorers (Outriders/jump Intercessors) with my own fast
units. Win the mission, not the flamer fight.

## Data caveats
Detachment rules + Immolation Protocols are faction-pack-sourced (not in BSData).
Flamestorm cannon **S6 AP-2 D2** re-verified from `sm11.json` (this corrects the
older doc). List shape (Vulkan + ~20 pyreblasters + 2 Redeemers) is a pre-Dataslate
GT template — treat ratios as signal. If the build swaps in Librarius Conclave for
a second detachment, layer in the Discipline buffs (Divination re-rolls / Pyromancy
Sustained) per the SM-rules note; the base Forgefather's Seekers verdict stands.
