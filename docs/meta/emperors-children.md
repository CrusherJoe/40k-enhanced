# Emperor's Children — Frenzied Host + Court of the Phoenician (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

> Army rule **Thrill Seekers**: EC units **advance and still charge** (with re-roll support) —
> best-in-class delivery for an all-melee army. Assume the beatstick reaches you turn 1-2.

## The list & tapestry
An **elite MELEE** deathstar built around **Fulgrim** and the **Court of the Phoenician**, run
in the **Frenzied Host** detachment. The whole point is to put a near-un-killable duellist into
your line and have it delete a unit per turn while the supporting elites carve the rest.
- **Fulgrim** — **T11 W16 2+/4++, −1 to be hit**, **Fights First**. His Fight-phase strat
  **deletes/degrades one enemy unit within range**, and he shrugs shooting (2+/4++/−1hit means
  guns bounce). Verified: **Flawless Blades units have Fights First** (ec_fp) and Fulgrim's court
  strat targets a Fulgrim unit.
- **Defiler** — Daemon-Vehicle brawler (T-heavy, **5++**), a second Questoris-cracker.
- **Flawless Blades** infantry — **AP-4** melee, Fights First, the volume that clears screens
  and softens my bodies for the beatstick.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Fulgrim into the no-melee-invuln Questoris (3+ save only)** — Rotate can't help in melee; his
  Fight-phase deletion + Fights First means he swings first and can remove a Knight/turn.
- **Defiler** melee — a hull-cracker that trades with a Knight.
- **Flawless Blades AP-4** — strips armour to the bare 3+/no-invuln in the fight phase; with
  Thrill Seekers they arrive on turn 1-2.
- Note: EC's damage is **quality melee**, not invuln-negation shooting — a lone Lancer's 4++
  actually holds here.

## Anti-Sister threats
- **Fulgrim + Flawless Blades melee** is a mass grave for T3 W1 Sv3+/6++ Sisters — AP-4 and
  Fights First mean the girls die before they punch, and glass has no answer to being out-fought.
- **Thrill Seekers advance-and-charge** denies the kiting distance Sisters need.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: EXPECT A LOSS, but winnable.** This is one of the genuinely hard melee matchups — but
the anti-Knight is *concentrated in Fulgrim*, and the rest of the list is killable support.

- **Deciding factor:** do NOT feed Fulgrim. He's near-un-killable (T11 W16 2+/4++ −1hit shrugs my
  guns) and deletes whatever he touches — so I refuse the duel and win everywhere else.
- **HEIST:** **Screen and concede Fulgrim** (chaff-block his charge lanes, sacrifice a throwaway
  unit if he must eat something), then **shoot the killable support** — the Defiler (Volcano/
  thermal), Flawless Blades, and any court characters — with my volume guns. **Out-OC on Priority
  Assets**: my OC10 Questoris out-holds his few high-quality bodies. Lancer (4++ full invuln)
  counter-charges the Defiler / duels the killable elites, NOT Fulgrim. Win on POINTS, not kills.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
**Verdict: hard — the worst of the melee matchups (~40/60 for even the best build).** Glass gets
butchered by elite Fights-First melee and EC out-bodies fragile screens.
- **Plan:** treat it as **tarpit + out-score, never a fight**. W1 bodies WASTE Fulgrim's
  multi-damage (S14 D6+1 kills only ~4/swing); feed him disposable Arco/Sacresant tarpits to
  waste his activation, metabolize casualties into Miracle Dice / Blood-of-Martyrs, and out-TEMPO
  on the objective. Shoot the *killable* support (Defiler ~melta target, Flawless Blades with
  volume). Practice this matchup — it and DA-Ravenwing are the two Sisters losses to drill.

## Data caveats
- Fulgrim's exact strat wording and profile are FP/codex-sourced; BSData carries no stratagem
  text. Fights-First-on-Flawless-Blades and the Fulgrim court strat are verified in ec_fp.
- Verdicts assume a Valourstrike Knight list (Rotate = one Knight 4++/phase, shooting only —
  useless vs Fulgrim's melee) + ~1 Cerastus Lancer + Dominus.
- Sisters build referenced = Hallowed Martyrs (the recommended fresh list); EC is its explicit
  practice-loss matchup.
