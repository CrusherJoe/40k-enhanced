# Grey Knights — Banishers / Argent Assault (11E, PRELIMINARY, sourced 2026-07-26)

> **⚠ PRELIMINARY — NOT deep-analyzed.** We sourced GK vs BSData/wh40k-11e (`gk11.json` +
> `gk_fp.txt`, which confirms the **Banishers** and **Argent Assault** detachments and a
> Paladin/Terminator teleport-elite core) but have not run the per-phase matchup math. Treat the
> reads below as a first-pass expectation, not a verdict. **Needs a full pass** before a hard game.

## The list & tapestry
- **Argent Assault / Banishers** = a **psychic + teleport Terminator elite**: Paladins / Terminators
  at **2+ / 4++** with **Personal Teleporters** (deep strike + reposition), psychic mortal-wound
  output, and per-phase invuln/charge buffs (e.g. Paladin +1-to-charge, ward stratagems). Low model
  count, extreme quality — the elite-brick pole with mobility and MW attached.
- Force-multiplier characters (Grand Master Voldus / Librarians) drive the psychic + buff layer.

## Anti-Knight threats (preliminary)
- **Psychic mortal wounds** skip the Ion Shield and the 4++ entirely — the recurring invuln-negation
  lever that beats Knights.
- **Teleport + charge into the no-melee-invuln fight phase:** 2+/4++ Terminator melee (Nemesis force
  weapons, likely S6-8 AP-2/-3 + MW) cracks a Questoris that only has a 3+ save in melee.
- Deep strike mobility denies clean screening — a lone Knight can be reached.

## Anti-Sister threats (preliminary)
- Teleporting 2+/4++ Terminators with MW + force-weapon melee butcher T3 W1 bodies on the drop;
  psychic MW ignores cover and saves. Elite-but-few, so likely low OC — but very hard to shoot off.

## How Imperial Knights fare (preliminary)
- **Likely COIN-FLIP / mildly UNFAVOURABLE.** Deciding factor (expected): **psychic MW + teleport
  melee** bypass the Ion Shield and the fight-phase 3+ save. Provisional HEIST: **Lancer 4++
  counter-charge** (the only melee-resistant piece) + **assassinate Voldus/Librarians** to blank the
  psychic/buff layer; screen the teleport; and — like other elite bricks — **out-OC** the few bodies
  on the mission. Confirm with a real pass; the MW volume vs a Rotated Knight is the open question.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare (preliminary)
- **Likely HARD for Sisters.** A 2+/4++ elite that also carries MW and deep-strike mobility is
  exactly the profile Sisters struggle with: hard to shoot off (68% of 4++ fails, but 2+ armour +
  4++ is durable per model), MW ignores my saves, and the drop reaches my glass line. Provisional
  plan: Hallowed Martyrs — tarpit the teleport (Arco/Sacresants), grind AP-1/2 volume + Miracle Dice
  into the bricks, and out-SCORE the low body count rather than out-kill it. Needs verification.
