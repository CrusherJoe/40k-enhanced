# Chaos Space Marines — Renegade Raiders (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

> Army rule **Dark Pacts**: any CSM unit can swap to **[LETHAL HITS]** for a phase by
> taking a Leadership test — fail and the unit takes **D3 mortal wounds**. Free crit-wounds
> at a small self-damage cost; it's on every unit but doesn't bypass invulns.

## The list & tapestry
**Renegade Raiders** — mobile, objective-grabbing raiders. Detachment rule **"Raiders and
Reavers"** gives units **[ASSAULT]** and **+1 AP** while near objectives (advance-and-shoot,
then bite harder where it matters). The anti-Knight hammer is a **Vashtorr + Defiler** package:
- **Vashtorr the Arkifane** — his hammer is **[ANTI-VEHICLE 4+, DEVASTATING WOUNDS]** (verified
  chaos_fp): wounds my Knights on 4s *regardless of Strength* AND crit-wounds skip the invuln.
  Also projects a **+2 Strength aura** to a nearby **Daemon-Vehicle Defiler**, turning it into a
  real Questoris-cracker.
- **Lord Discordant on Helstalker** (M14") — the mortal-wound faucet / buff piece and a fast
  melee threat.
- Supported by Daemon-Engine vehicles (Defiler, autocannon platforms) and cultist/marine chaff.

This is a **concentrated** anti-Knight package (two named multipliers + one buffed Defiler), not
a saturated one — which is exactly why it grades as winnable.

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Vashtorr's hammer** — [ANTI-VEHICLE 4+, DEV WOUNDS] both bypasses the Ion Shield (Dev) and
  wounds a Questoris on 4s. The single scariest hit in the list.
- **Defiler under Vashtorr's +2S aura** — a Daemon-Vehicle brawler that reaches Knight-killing
  Strength only *with* the aura; break the aura (kill/displace Vashtorr) and it drops off.
- **Dark Pacts [LETHAL HITS]** on the shooting/melee that matters (autocannons, melee claws) —
  more auto-wounds into my hull.
- Melee into the **no-melee-invuln Questoris (3+ save only)** — Lord Disco + Defiler fights.

## Anti-Sister threats
- **Vashtorr / Defiler / Lord Disco melee** shreds T3 W1 bodies (multi-damage is wasted on W1,
  but AP + volume still clears squads).
- **+1 AP near objectives** strips the 3+ armour of glass Sisters exactly where they're scoring.
- **Dark Pacts Lethal Hits** on bolter/autocannon volume auto-wounds W1 infantry.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: COIN-FLIP (~50/50), a winnable kill-race heist.** The anti-Knight is thin and
*concentrated in killable models* — the good outcome for a ~6-9-model Knight army.

- **Deciding factor:** whether I remove the multipliers before they remove a Knight. Vashtorr +
  Lord Discordant are the whole engine; kill them and the Defiler deflates (no +2S aura) and the
  MW faucet stops.
- **HEIST:** **Assassinate Vashtorr and the Lord Discordant early** with Volcano/thermal +
  a Cerastus Lancer counter-charge (its **4++ full invuln** tanks the Defiler and lets it hunt
  characters). Rotate the ONE Knight they focus each phase. Play the mission — out-OC their
  bodies on Purge/Priority Assets — and win the kill-race once the two named pieces are gone.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
**Verdict: hard-ish but playable.** Glass T3 W1 Sv3+/6++ gets butchered in melee, but the melee
threats are FEW models — shoot them down before they land.
- **Plan:** massed AP-1/2 bolter+melta VOLUME + Miracle Dice to drop Vashtorr/Defiler/Lord Disco
  as they close (melta the Defiler; W1 bodies WASTE their multi-damage). Screen the drop, tarpit
  with Arco/Sacresants, and out-score on the objective — never try to out-fight them.

## Data caveats
- **Other CSM archetypes in the feed — do NOT conflate their rules:**
  - **Huron's Marauders** (the HeeYaw "Longest Night" list): detachment rule is **"Tyrannical
    Motivation"**, a *Command-phase-selectable* buff to a Heretic-Astartes **Infantry** unit —
    NOT "Raiders and Reavers." My earlier bank mis-attributed the +1-AP-near-objectives rule to
    it; the favourable verdict vs that list is unchanged (the HeeYaw win was 90-61, played WITHOUT
    Rotate Ion Shields).
  - **Murdertalon Raiders** — new, appeared in the feed; not yet analysed. Note-only; grade when
    a list is available.
- Detachment stratagem text (incl. exact "Raiders and Reavers" wording) is FP/codex-sourced —
  BSData carries no stratagem text.
- Verdicts assume a Valourstrike Knight list (Rotate Ion Shields = one Knight 4++/phase) + ~1
  Cerastus Lancer + Dominus.
