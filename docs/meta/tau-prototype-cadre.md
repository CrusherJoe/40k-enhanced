# T'au — Experimental Prototype Cadre + Kauyon (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

> The anti-Knight shooting ceiling of the meta. Markerlight-fed AP volume + railgun
> Devastating Wounds + Farsight fusion + army-wide evasion. **UNFAVOURABLE for
> Knights** (the one gunline built to out-shoot a Knight), hard-but-survivable for
> Sisters (AP volume bleeds them, but melta one-shots the no-invuln Hammerheads).

---

## The list & tapestry
- **Detachment:** **Experimental Prototype Cadre**, running the **Kauyon** stratagem/rule (the "patient hunter" turn — army-wide re-roll to hit, timed to a focus-fire round). **Disposition:** typically Purge / Take and Hold. Record: top-table 5-0/4-1 finisher.
- **Army rule — For the Greater Good / Markerlights:** a marked unit is easier to hit (removes hit penalties / grants re-rolls / +1 to hit depending on markerlight count) — this is the **force-multiplier that converts T'au VOLUME into anti-Knight lethality**. Kill the markerlight sources (Pathfinders / drones / Stealth suits) and the gunline drops a tier.
- **Anti-Knight guns at ceiling (verified BSData):**
  - **Railgun (Hammerhead)** — 72" A1 S20 AP-5 **D6+6 [Devastating Wounds, Heavy]** → crit-wound = mortals that SKIP my invuln entirely; a marked/Kauyon railgun reliably chunks a Knight.
  - **Heavy rail rifle (Broadside)** — 60" A2 S12 AP-4 **D6+1 [Heavy, Devastating Wounds]**.
  - **Rail rifle (Pathfinders)** — 30" A1 S10 AP-4 D3 [Devastating Wounds, Heavy].
  - **Ion cannon (Hammerhead, overcharge)** — 60" A(D6+3) S8 AP-2 D3 [Blast] — AP-2 volume.
  - **Fusion (Farsight bomb / Crisis suits):** fusion blaster 12" A1 S9 AP-4 D6 [Melta 2]; Commander high-energy fusion 18" A1 S10 AP-4 D6 [Melta 2]; Piranha fusion 12" S9 AP-4 D6 [Melta 4] — deep-strike/mobile alpha that melts a Knight up close.
- **Evasion:** army-wide fall-back-and-shoot / Fly / defensive support — **neuters my melee** (they don't sit still to be charged; a fell-back T'au unit still shoots, my fell-back Knight can't).

## Anti-Knight threats
- **Railgun Dev Wounds** is the marquee threat: S20 AP-5 D6+6, and the crit-wound → mortal path **bypasses the 5++/4++ Rotate entirely** (invuln-negation, not just AP).
- **Markerlight-fed AP-2+ volume** (ion cannons, burst arrays, missile pods) drowns the 68%-failing Ion Shield across multiple Knights — Rotate saves only one.
- **Farsight fusion bomb** (AP-4 Melta deep strike) melts a Knight from <12" if I let it land in half-range.
- **Evasion** means my Lancer/Gallant can't reliably catch and duel — the melee heist that beats other armies is blunted here.

## Anti-Sister threats
- **Massed AP-1/AP-2 volume** (burst cannons S5-6 AP-1, ion, missile pods) is the exact profile that kills T3 W1 Sisters — one dead body per unsaved wound; the gunline out-ranges and out-shoots her.
- **High-AP/high-D rail weapons are WASTED on W1 chaff** (D6+6 into a Sister is grotesque overkill) — but they delete her durable pieces (Paragons, Castigators, Immolators) and the AP volume grinds the bodies.

## How Imperial Knights fare
- **UNFAVOURABLE.** Deciding factor: this is the meta's dedicated anti-Knight gunline — **railgun Dev Wounds bypass my invuln, markerlight volume drowns it, and evasion denies my melee answer.** Rotate saves one Knight; the marked-and-Kauyon focus round removes another. THE HEIST: **kill the FORCE-MULTIPLIER (markerlights) and the platforms EARLY** — Volcano/thermal the Hammerheads and Broadsides (they have **no invuln** → my big guns one-shot/one-round them; math ≈ Hammerhead 33.5 damage from a Sisters melta stack, worse for a Volcano only because of overkill efficiency, still lethal), delete Pathfinders/drones to strip the mark-engine, deny the fusion deep strike with screening + the Navigator dome (drops must land >8-9"), Rotate the focused Knight, and race mobility secondaries. Realistic result is a loss — play for a strong score, not the win.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
- **HARD (AP volume) but MORE tractable than for Knights.** Deciding factor: Sisters BLEED to the AP-1/2 firehose but don't evaporate (6++ floor + Miracle Dice + body count), and crucially the T'au armour has **NO invuln** — **melta one-shots a Hammerhead** (railgun platform) and cracks Broadsides. THE PLAN: win the MISSION — soften-then-delete the no-invuln rail/ion platforms with the Vahl+Paragon melta hammer (strip cover via Immolator Purge & Cleanse, +2 AP via Castigator), clear markerlight sources and drones with volume/blast, use HIDDEN/Gone-to-Ground home-holders (Sisters CAN be Hidden — untargetable beyond detection range when not shooting), and out-tempo on secondaries. Expect to be behind on the shooting war, ahead on the board.
