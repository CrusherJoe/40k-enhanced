# Imperial Fists — "Great Value" Emperor's Shield + Librarius Conclave (11E, verified 2026-07-26)

The friend's LSO list — **the specific target the whole Knight project is built
against.** Mono-Codex Imperial Fists, 3 DP = **Emperor's Shield (2DP) + Librarius
Conclave (1DP)**, ~1985 pts, disposition Purge the Foe. Stats from BSData/wh40k-11e
(`sm11.json` + IF/Emperor's-Shield/Conclave in the SM faction pack v1.1).

## The list & tapestry
A reserve-heavy teleport/deep-strike alpha built on an **OC34
Terminator brick** (Lysander + Ancient), with Oath of Moment as the convergence engine.

| Unit | Stats | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **TH/SS Assault Terminators (10) + Lysander + Ancient** | 2+/**4++**, **OC34** | the brick. Fist of Dorn S10 AP-3 D3 A5 **Dev**; TH; ~**24-28 melee → one-rounds ANY Knight** (even Lancer 4++). No psyker; M5 + Teleport Homer |
| **Cyclone Terminators (10) + Librarian [Fusillade]** | 2+/4++ | **#1 ranged Knight-threat**: krak S9 **AP-2** + cyclone missiles; ~13/big Knight, **~14 one-shots an Armiger**. *Fury of the First* +1 hit vs Oath. Fusillade = **Lethal Hits** |

**Tapestry threads — the whole army converges on ONE Knight/turn:**
- **Oath of Moment** (army rule): name 1 of my units → army-wide **re-roll Hits +
  +1 to Wound** into it (mono-IF gets the +1). **~doubles** every unit's output into
  the Oathed Knight: Speeders 4.6→10.7, cyclones 6.4→11.5, Sternguard 4.2→8.1.
- **Wrath of Dorn** (Emperor's Shield rule): re-roll a **Wound-of-1 army-wide** +
  **FULL wound re-roll for Lysander's unit**.
- **The OC34 brick — Inspiring Commander + Astartes Banner**: Lysander's Inspiring
  Commander **sets** non-Character Terminators to **OC2** (army-wide — the cyclone
  brick benefits too); the Ancient's **Astartes Banner** then adds **+1 OC to every
  model in its unit** → **10 Terminators OC3 + Lysander OC2 + Ancient OC2 = OC34**
  (earlier undercounted as OC22 by missing the banner). **Not shock-immune**, but it
  only tests below half-strength (6+ dead) unless an enemy FORCES a test — and a Knight
  army has no forcer, so for me it's effectively permanent (a shock-weaponizer like
  Tyranids/Necrons/CSM/Daemons CAN drop it). + *Icon of Obstinacy* (−1 to wound at S≥T5)
  + Rampart (2+ inv once).
- **Emperor's Shield stratagems**: **Armour of Contempt** (−1 AP def — drops my
  Avenger AP-2→AP-1, saves his Intercessors), Fury of the First (+1 hit/+1 wound),
  Disciplined Extermination (+1 AP + Ignore Cover), **Dropship Extraction** (2nd
  teleport engine — bail any Terminator unit to reserves), **Wrathful Conquerors**
  (make a brick's objective sticky).
- **Librarius Conclave**: pick 1 Discipline/round, all his psyker-led units (Sternguard
  + cyclone Termies) get it — Divination (rr-1s hit&wound) / Pyromancy (+1 AP +
  Sustained w/ Fusillade) / Telekinesis (Deep-Strike-enable + −1 S def) / etc.

**Full-stack alpha into an Oathed Castellan ≈ 30-34 dmg (~10 of it unsaveable
Sternguard Dev) → kills a healthy 28W Castellan in a turn.** The melee brick
(Lysander unit, full Wrath re-roll + Oath +1) ≈ 49 → one-rounds ANY Knight.

## Anti-Knight threats
Ranked by expected dmg/turn into a big Knight (all buffs stacked): **Land Speeders**
(Oath ~doubles them, one-shot Armigers, mobile — but Melta bonus needs <9", so a DS
arrival >9" only does ~6-9); **cyclone Termies** (persistent AP-2 + Lethal); **Sternguard**
(the ~7/turn unsaveable Dev chipper — their teleport turn is their *weakest* shoot);
**TH/SS brick** (melee, one-rounds anything, but M5, must walk/homer in). Everything
threatens through **convergence on the Oathed target**, not raw durability.

## How Imperial Knights fare
**Verdict: COIN-FLIP** (~5-5; the hardened 2-Lancer sim lands even). Start from the
**expectation of a loss** and hunt the heist — do NOT assume the guns bail me out.
Deciding factor: **whether I break his convergence before it deletes a Knight, and
whether I out-score two slow M5 bricks (OC34 + ~OC21) I can't out-OC head-on.**

**HEIST:**
1. **Navigator's 12" anti-DS dome is the linchpin** — it denies all four arrival
   threats (Sternguard teleport, both Speeder drops, homer jumps all must land
   outside 12"). Keep the Navigator; tuck home bodies within ~3" of it.
2. **Break the convergence — kill a LEG.** Oath hits 1 target/turn, so **bait it
   onto a cheap/hurt Armiger** and **spread**. **Kill Land Speeders FIRST** (Oath
   doubles them + one-shot Armigers + mobile leg; Volcano one-shots one), then
   cyclone Termies, then Sternguard. Remove any leg → the alpha drops below a
   Castellan's wounds. Kill Sternguard T1 if on-board (Avenger + Helverins + plasma
   ≈ 7 dead).
3. **Never melee the hammers with a premium Knight** (Dev Wounds bypass the 4++) —
   pin with expendable Armigers/kiting; his M5 brick can't catch a back-corner
   gunline (Super-Heavy Walker = fall-back-and-still-shoot).
4. **Rotate the one Oathed Knight** each turn (4++); take cover off ruin walls (his
   ranged into my in-cover Knights is −1).
5. **Out-score on mobility.** His two bricks are slow and his objectives sticky
   (Objective Secured + Wrathful Conquerors), so I **can't just show up after he
   leaves** — I win by ATTRITION: kill his soft OC (Speeders/Sternguard/Vanguard =
   OC removed) so his board control collapses to the 2 bricks by T3-5 while my
   mobile pieces take the flanks. **Cede the ONE central objective the OC34 brick
   squats; hold the others.** The +10 home-steal is UNRELIABLE (need OC>10 to flip
   his sticky Intercessors) — don't make the plan depend on it.

**Current counter-list:** `examples/knights-vs-greatvalue.yaml` = **Valourstrike
Lance (Rotate + Advance-and-shoot) + Dominus Foebreakers (Priority Assets, +1 hit
vs terrain-sitters)** — 2 Dominus Castellans + Crusader + 2 Helverins + Warglaive +
Navigator + Immolator cover-strip + BS home-holder. Plan: `docs/knights-vs-greatvalue-plan.md`,
whole-game sim `docs/Great-Value-vs-Knights-Full-Game-Simulation.docx`.

**Honest reality-check:** earlier passes over-sold this (~78-44, ~74-50) by leaning
on an unreliable home-steal and treating his OC as brick-only — but **all his models
have OC** and his defensive game (army-wide + sticky + Armour of Contempt + Hidden
deploy) is strong. Realistic honest result ≈ **narrow Knight win / coin-flip**, and
the triple-gun standoff may be **too light on mobile OC** to close (open question:
trade a Castellan for 2 more Armigers). Win by **refusing the fight the wall wants**,
deleting his soft legs from safety, and out-mobiling M5 bricks on the mission — not
by out-fighting or out-tanking him.

## How Adepta Sororitas fare
Harder for Sisters than for Knights. The **OC34 brick is exactly the
elite-Terminator archetype that beats glass T3 W1** (it tabled the user's Sisters
98-31 in a comparable HeeYaw game), and Sternguard Dev + AP-2 cyclone volume shred
W1 bodies. BUT Sisters answer the **deep-strike/teleport alpha** better than Knights:
massed AP-1/-2 bolter + melta VOLUME + Miracle Dice shoots each brick down as it
lands (Sternguard's −1 is melee-only; melta ignores it). Play tarpit + out-score
(Sacresants/Arco tarpits waste his activations), melta the Speeders, never try to
out-OC the brick — concede its objective, out-mobile the rest.
