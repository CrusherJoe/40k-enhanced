# Battle plan — Knights vs "Great Value" (Emperor's Shield Imperial Fists)

Practice game for the LSO. All data verified 11E (BSData/wh40k-11e + SM Faction Pack v1.1).
List: `examples/knights-vs-greatvalue.yaml` (Valourstrike + Dominus, **Purge the Foe**).
Vs his Purge → you play **Meatgrinder** (kill-differential + hold + his home).

## What he is (and the ONE thing that beats him)
**~155 wounds of 2+/4++** — 10 TH/SS Terminators (T5 W4) + 10 Terminators (T5 W3) + Lysander (T5 W7)
+ 6 Bladeguard + 10 Sternguard + 10 Vanguard + Intercessors + 2 Land Speeders. **Everything is OC1**
(one unit OC2). Emperor's Shield adds **no save-buff** (Wrath of Dorn is *offensive* — re-roll wounds
vs his Oath target, full re-roll for Lysander's unit). Librarius Conclave picks **one Discipline/round**
(Telekinesis = the switch for Temporal Corridor **Deep Strike** + −1 S to wound his psyker units;
Biomancy +2"M; Divination re-roll 1s; Pyromancy +1 AP + Sustained; Telepathy ignore-hit-mods).

**His mobility is NOT one teleporting brick — it's four separate arrival threats:**
- **Sternguard (10) + Librarian [Temporal Corridor]** — the **#1 ranged Knight-killer.** Bolt rifles are
  **Devastating Wounds**: a mass volley **crit-wounds straight past your 4++/5++** (Dev Wounds = the
  wound pool ignores invulns). And they **teleport** (bail to Reserves unengaged → Deep Strike back). So
  they appear on a fresh angle and dump invuln-ignoring wounds into a bracketed Knight.
- **Terminator Squad (10, storm bolters + 2 cyclone launchers) + Librarian [Fusillade]** — **THIS** is the
  Fusillade unit (Lethal Hits, +Sustained under Pyromancy). Cyclones (krak S9) = ranged anti-Knight;
  storm bolters = anti-infantry. Slow (M5) but has a Teleport Homer.
- **TH/SS Assault Terminators (10) + Lysander + Ancient** — **no psyker, walks M5**, so it can't
  self-teleport — **but it has a Teleport Homer** (token placed mid-board at start → it Deep Strikes there
  once). Thunder Hammers + Fist of Dorn are **Dev Wounds** — one-round any Knight it reaches (~40+ Oathed).
- **2× Land Speeder** — multi-melta **S9 AP-4 D6 Melta 2** on an M14 FLY chassis that arrives from reserve.
  One won't drop a Knight, but **two multi-meltas into a bracketed Knight's rear is a real kill** —
  "melta of any kind is dangerous."

**You cannot table 155W of 2+/4++. Don't try. He is OC1 — you WIN BY OUT-SCORING while the Navigator dome
denies his four arrival threats and you refuse to feed the hammers.**

## Three hard rules (from the real 11E rules)
1. **NEVER melee the hammers with a premium Knight.** TH/SS + Fist of Dorn are **Dev Wounds** — they bypass
   your 4++/5++ and do ~40+ (Oathed) in a round. Even the Lancer dies. Pin/screen the brick only with
   **expendable Armigers** (140 to eat a round), never a big Knight.
2. **The Navigator's 12" anti-Deep-Strike dome is the linchpin.** It denies ALL four arrival threats
   near your key units/home: Sternguard teleport, Land Speeder drops, and both Terminator bricks' Teleport
   Homer jumps must land **outside** the dome. Keep it over your scoring. (Temporal Corridor also needs the
   Sternguard **unengaged** to bail — tag them with an Armiger and they can't leave.)
3. **The Sternguard are the real ranged Knight-killer — mass Dev Wounds ignore your invulns.** If they're
   on the board Turn 1, **delete them T1** (Avenger + Helverin + plasma + RFBC). If he holds them in
   reserve to teleport, keep the dome up and **spread your Knights so one fresh volley can't focus a
   bracketed one** — then kill them the turn they land. The cyclone Termies + Land Speeder melta are the
   other two ranged threats; everything else only hurts a Knight in melee.

## The list (1985/2000)
- **Knight Castellan** [Blessed Plate, T13] — Volcano *chunks Lysander* (6.4/7W), plasma decimator (D3)
  kills W3 Terminators. Durable mid-board anchor, OC10.
- **Cerastus Knight Lancer** — **NOT for the brick.** Fast (M14) 4++ that **hunts the backline**
  (Sternguard survivors, characters, his home) — D8 lance one-shots characters. Stays out of the hammers.
- **Knight Crusader** [RFBC] — Avenger (A18) kills Sternguard/W3; thermal (D6 melta) kills W4 Terminators.
- **2× Armiger Warglaive** — thermal spears (D6 melta) grind the brick from range; the *screen/pin bodies*.
- **Armiger Helverin** — autocannons into Sternguard/Bladeguard.
- **Navigator** [ally] — the **anti-Deep-Strike dome** over your home; denies Temporal Corridor re-drops.
- **Battle Sisters + Immolator** [ally, Requisitioned] — forced split → **OC10 Sisters half holds home**
  under the dome; melta half + Immolator **twin-multi-melta** (not flamers) grind Terminators; Immolator
  **Purge & Cleanse = +33%** into whatever you focus.

## Deployment
- **Home = OC10 Battle Sisters half under the Navigator dome.** That objective is locked; his teleport
  can't land near it, and OC10 shrugs footslog contest. (Your home scores *you* nothing under Purge, but
  it denies *him* 4-5 VP.)
- **Castellan + Crusader central-mid** with LOS — grind from range, hold the middle at OC10-while-shooting.
- **Armigers spread forward** as screens/OC + ranged grind; keep them cheap-tradeable, not clumped.
- **Lancer on a flank**, angled at his backline (Sternguard survivors / characters), NOT the brick.
- **Two Teleport Homer tokens go down at start** (both Terminator bricks) — expect a mid-board terminator
  drop. Leave no 9" gaps near your key units **outside** the dome; keep the dome over what you actually
  need to hold, and screen the open mid-board with Armiger bodies so a homer drop can't land clean + charge.

## Turn by turn
**T1 — kill the Sternguard (his best ranged Knight-threat).** If they're on the board, Avenger + Helverin
+ Castellan plasma + RFBC delete the 10 Sternguard before they can teleport + dump invuln-ignoring Dev
Wounds. If he reserved them, spend that fire on the cyclone Termies / whatever's exposed and keep the dome
tight. Lancer repositions toward his flank/backline. Castellan/Crusader chip the nearest brick from range.
Grab the central objectives with OC10 Knights.
**T2 — grind + out-position.** Volcano onto Lysander (chunk him, finish next turn). Thermal + spears +
Immo twin-MM (cover-strip'd) into the TH/SS brick — ~6-8 Terminators over T1-2. Lancer dives the backline
(characters / his home). **If the brick charges you, receive it with an ARMIGER** (pins it → no teleport
→ eats the Dev-Wound hammers for a round; a 140-pt trade for a 360-pt brick's turn).
**T3-4 — score the crater.** He's bleeding Terminators and can't out-OC you; Meatgrinder rewards your
kill-differential (dead Sternguard/Bladeguard/Terminators + his home) while he can't table your durable
Knights. Keep feeding cheap Armigers to pin the brick if it's near your scoring; Lancer takes his home.
**T5 — close.** Hold central + expansion, contest his home, tally the kill-differential.

## Watch-outs
- **Wrath of Dorn:** his Oath target eats re-rolled wounds in melee — so his brick hits *whatever he
  Oaths* extra hard. Don't leave a lone Knight in charge range of the brick to be Oathed + deleted.
- **His Discipline is a per-round choice:** teleport turn (Telekinesis) = he gives up Divination re-rolls
  and Biomancy speed that round — punish the trade. Telekinesis also gives his psyker units **−1 S to be
  wounded** — so on that turn your S9 melta wounds his terminators on 3+ still (unchanged), fine.
- **Sternguard Dev-Wound math:** a 10-body volley (~20 shots) crit-wounds on unmodified 6s → those wounds
  **skip your invuln**. Buffed (Divination re-roll 1s / Lethal from an Oath) it's a genuine bracket's worth
  of damage into one Knight. Don't let a bracketed (degraded) Knight sit in their teleport arc alone.
- **Land Speeders (2× multi-melta S9 AP-4 D6 Melta 2, M14 FLY, arrive from reserve):** the mobile melta
  that snipes your Knights' rear. One won't kill; **two into a bracketed Knight will.** Dome-deny their
  drop, and kill them with Helverin autocannons / Avenger the turn they show — don't leave a hurt Knight
  exposed to both.
- **Teleport Homers:** even the M5 TH/SS brick can jump mid-board once. A mid-board Armiger screen + the
  dome force the drop far from your scoring; then it still has to *walk/charge* in — kite it.

## The five reminders (table card)
1. **Out-score, don't out-kill** — he's OC1; you can't table 155W of 2+/4++.
2. **Sternguard = mass Dev Wounds that ignore your invulns.** Kill them T1 if on board; if reserved,
   **spread Knights so a fresh volley can't focus a bracketed one**, then kill on arrival.
3. **NEVER melee the hammers with a big Knight** (Dev-Wound TH/SS + Fist of Dorn bypass invulns) — pin
   with Armigers.
4. **Navigator dome is the linchpin** — it denies Sternguard teleport, Land Speeder melta drops, AND both
   Teleport-Homer jumps. Keep it over your scoring.
5. **Lancer hunts the backline, not the hammers.** Screen the mid-board (homers) with Armiger bodies.
