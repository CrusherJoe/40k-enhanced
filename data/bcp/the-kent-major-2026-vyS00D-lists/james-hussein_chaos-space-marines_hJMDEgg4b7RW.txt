The Ebon Host marches from the warp‑forges of Ghal’Zharod, a warband bound in uneasy alliance with Vashtorr the Arkifane. Guided by the Iron Revenant’s cold, mechanical will, the Black Legion unleashes a tide of daemon engines, corrupted warriors, and fanatical cultists to harvest souls and machinery for Vashtorr’s growing lattice of warp‑tech abominations.
Warpsmith Vhal’kar, Forge‑Binder of the Ebon Host, drives the host forward with brutal efficiency, awakening ancient Defilers, unleashing skittering Venomcrawlers, and binding tortured champions into Helbrute sarcophagi. Biker outriders race ahead like hunting beasts, while Red Corsairs raiders join the slaughter for plunder and favour.
Where this warhost descends, machine‑spirits scream, data‑vaults collapse, and the boundary between flesh and metal dissolves. Worlds do not merely fall — they are consumed, piece by piece, in service to the Long War and the Arkifane’s unfathomable design.
