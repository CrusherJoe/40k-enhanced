As the bartender called last call, Chris raised his glass one final time. Connor and Daren followed suit without needing another speech. "To Mean Machine," Chris declared. "And may the Wolf continue his incredible undefeated streak... of never actually showing up." Three glasses clinked together beneath the neon beer signs, and somewhere in that dingy little dive bar, the Gateway Open's most dangerous collection of budget ballers and lovable degenerates was officially born.
