Part 7

Daren stared at the imaginary roster they'd built between empty glasses and bad decisions. Mean Machine wasn't the youngest team. It definitely wasn't the richest team. It probably wasn't even the most organized team. But every single one of them knew how to laugh through bad dice, celebrate impossible victories, and mercilessly roast each other before, during, and after every game. They weren't chasing glory so much as another legendary weekend full of stories that would somehow get funnier every time they were retold.
