Then the doors swung open, and in walked Daren and JT, The Mean Machine. They looked like two guys who had just punched out after a ten-hour shift welding steel, grabbed a gas station breakfast, and decided they'd conquer the galaxy before happy hour. Their army cases had more dents than a '92 Chevy pickup, and each scratch had "a story" that somehow always started with, "Back before they ruined the rules..." JT carried a coffee thermos that everyone suspected hadn't actually held coffee since the Obama administration. Daren insisted his lucky dice had been rolling sixes since Second Edition, even though half of them had the paint rubbed off. The two spent twenty minutes arguing about deployment zones, fifteen minutes complaining that "terrain used to mean something," and five minutes trying to figure out who forgot the reading glasses. They chirped each other nonstop like an old married couple, swore every bad dice roll was because "these young kids don't respect the Machine," and somehow always managed to finish every story with, "Hell... let's get a beer after this."
