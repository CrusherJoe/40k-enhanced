Part 1

The first round was on the table before anyone even admitted what they were really there to talk about. Chris leaned back on a barstool that looked like it'd survived three bar fights and a flood, while his co-captain Connor stirred the ice in his whiskey like he was plotting a military campaign instead of a weekend of plastic toy soldiers. Across from them sat Daren, halfway through a questionable lager and pretending he didn't know exactly where the conversation was headed. The jukebox crackled, someone missed badly at darts, and Connor finally broke the silence. "The Gateway Open ain't gonna win itself. We need to assemble the Mean Machine."

Daren laughed into his beer. "Mean Machine? Sounds like a parole violation." Chris grinned. "Exactly. We don't have a superteam. We've got an Ol' Man Team with bad knees, questionable financial decisions, and just enough experience to know we should've quit this hobby years ago." Connor pointed a finger like a lawyer delivering closing arguments. "Ballers on a budget. We don't buy every new hotness. We buy what's on sale, paint it eventually, and convince ourselves it's been the meta all along." Daren shook his head, but the smile gave him away. He was already halfway convinced.
