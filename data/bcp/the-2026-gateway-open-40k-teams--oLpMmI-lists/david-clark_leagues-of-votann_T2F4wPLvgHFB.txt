For decades, Chris of the White Scars rode into battle beside David of the Leagues of Votann. Though one fought with lightning speed and the other with unwavering resolve, their victories became the stuff of legend. Across countless war zones, they trusted each other without question.

But even the strongest minds can be tempted.

At first, Chris noticed only subtle changes in David. The Kin spent longer consulting ancient ancestor cores, claiming they spoke of forgotten truths. Strange runes began appearing across his armor—symbols that no Votann record could identify. When questioned, David merely said, "The Ancestors have shown me a better path."

Chris felt unease.

Campaign after campaign, David grew colder. His judgment became ruthless, sacrificing allies for impossible gains. His once-practical wisdom twisted into obsession as whispers from beyond reality convinced him that the Warp was not an enemy, but a source of limitless knowledge waiting to be harnessed.

Chris confronted him within the shattered halls of an abandoned hold.

"These voices are not your Ancestors," Chris pleaded. "They're lies."

David looked up, his eyes glowing with unnatural light.

"No, old friend. They are freedom."

Their final battle erupted beneath the broken statues of forgotten Kin heroes. Chris fought with every ounce of speed and skill the White Scars possessed, desperately searching for the friend he had once known. But every strike was answered by impossible Warp-fueled power.

When David finally withdrew into a swirling rift of impossible colors, Chris made no attempt to pursue him. He knew the warrior disappearing into the Warp was no longer the steadfast Kin who had once stood beside him.

Years later, whenever new recruits asked why a place at the White Scars' feast hall remained empty, Chris would answer only once:

"It belongs to a friend who lost a battle no blade could win."

And though David's name would one day be cursed across the Imperium, Chris still whispered a prayer before every campaign—not for victory, but for the impossible hope that somewhere beneath the Warp's corruption, his old friend might still remember who he once was.
