DEFILER
Huge Daemon Engines dripping with corruption, the Defilers used
by the Death Guard are horrifically powerful whether bombarding
the foe from range with grotesquely diseased projectiles or tearing
them apart at close quarters with piston-driven claws of rusted
metal etched with cursed runes of corrosion.
