DEFILER
Infernal many-legged Daemon Engines of enormous size, Defilers
combine an arsenal of heavy weapons with brutal close-quarters
butchery. Those hurled into battle by the World Eaters are
rune-daubed prisons for ferocious warp entities that vent their
hate and fury upon the foe with cataclysmically gory results.
